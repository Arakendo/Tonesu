    ALI-001 · Alinsky, Rules for Radicals (1971) — The Tactical Rules

    S777 Power is both your actual force and what the adversary believes your directed power to be.

    S778 When your people operate outside their domain of competence, your collective power diminishes.

    S779 Force the adversary to operate outside their domain of competence and their power diminishes.

    S780 Force demands that the adversary act according to its own stated rules.

    S781 Ridicule is the most potent instrument available to human persons.

    S782 A tactic is good when the people who deploy it take pleasure in it.

    S783 A tactic that runs without temporal limit exhausts the enjoyment that sustains it.

    S784 The collective applies directed pressure to the adversary without relent.

    S785 The threat signal carries greater dread than the act of force itself.

    S786 Apply sufficient directed force toward a negative and it transmutes into a positive.

    S787 A victorious attack carries a structural requirement: the collective must build something.

    S788 Select a target; sever it from its support network; reduce it to one individual; make it the adversary.
