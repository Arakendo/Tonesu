    ADL-001 · Adversarial Designation and Action-Space Activation

    S881 Adversary-designation does not by itself change the action-boundary.

    S882 When adversary-designation is granted legitimacy, it changes the action-boundary.

    S883 Ideology designates an adversary / ideology designates a legitimized enemy.

    S884 Once ideology designates a legitimized enemy, it redraws what may be done.

    S885 Repeated legitimized-enemy designation makes directed power grow.

    S886 When the secret police designates a legitimized enemy, terror grows.

    S887 Under forceful circumstances and legitimized-enemy designation, ordinary persons bind themselves to coercive power.

    S888 The legitimized enemy operates by means of epistemic closure.

    S885 (ADL-001-E) go {la-to-su re si lo-wi-fe'de-li}, la-wi-ra be go {latosu re si lowife'deli}, lawira be Repetition stabilizes and grows coercive power

    S887 (ADL-001-G) go {la-go-su ne ra ; la-to-su si lo-wi-fe'de-li}, la-su-li ka-ne lo-wi-ra go {lagosu ne ra ; latosu si lowife'deli}, lasuli kane lowira Ordinary persons under structural pressure bind to coercive power
    S888 (ADL-001-H) la-wi-fe'de-li ne go-si wife'atofeka lawife'deli ne gosi wife'atofeka Enemy-designation stabilized through epistemic closure

    Key structural findings:

    Finding 1: designation and target-category separate cleanly. de-li names the adversarial target-role; de-li'si names the act of designation. This lets the corpus distinguish classifying an adversary from using that classification politically.

    Finding 2: the activation threshold is wi-fe + ka-fe. ADL-001-B/D show the decisive move: once designation acquires legitimacy (ne wi-fe or wi-fe'X), it changes the action-boundary (ki lo-ka-fe). That is the operational distinction between descriptive enemy-identification and coercive moral recategorization.

    Finding 3: repetition and institutions stabilize the system. ADL-001-E/F connect repeated designation and coercive institutions (mi-ne-su) to growth in wi-ra and wi-ra-ki. This gives the first explicit corpus test of how designation becomes durable rather than episodic.

    Finding 4: ordinary persons enter through structural pressure, not necessarily prior fanaticism. ADL-001-G bridges Browning's su-li / go-su analysis to designation systems: ordinary persons can bind themselves to coercive power when structural force and legitimized enemy-designation converge.

    Finding 5: wife'atofeka can stabilize legitimized enemyhood. ADL-001-H links enemy-designation to the self-legitimating closure established in WFE-001/002. This gives a formal answer to the contradiction-survival question: some labels persist not because they are never challenged, but because the challenge-space is already bounded.

    ADL-002 · Affect, Ritualization, and Public Consent

    S889 Adversary-designation is not itself legitimacy; legitimacy-conferring enemy-designation is.

    S890 Legitimized enemy-designation depends on atomization, not on hatred.

    S891 Ritual strengthens legitimacy-conferring enemy-designation.

    S892 Repeated media signaling of legitimacy-conferring enemy-designation grows public consent.

    S893 When ritual and media repetition stabilize the designation, the collective repeats the legitimized-enemy category itself.

    S894 Once a person is recategorized as a legitimized enemy, that person becomes rightless.

    S895 Once a person is recategorized as a legitimized enemy, that person is treated as outside political membership.

    S896 Even when the collective knows the designation operates through epistemic closure, the media still repeat it.

    Key structural findings:

    Finding 1: act-level enemy-designation is now explicit. ADL-001 distinguished target-category (de-li, wi-fe'de-li) from description. ADL-002 adds the label-act form wi-fe'de-li'si, which is the cleanest Tonesu handle on a legitimacy-conferring designation mechanism.

    Finding 2: affect intensifies but atomization stabilizes. ADL-002-B aligns enemy-designation with Arendt's mass-psychology logic: hatred (fa-ra-be) is not the primary structural requirement; atomized persons (no-ne-zo-li) are.

    Finding 3: ritual and media repetition convert designation into consent.     Finding 5: meta-awareness still fails when closure is in place. ADL-002-H extends WFE-002: even collective awareness of the closure mechanism does not by itself stop repeated public designation when wife'atofeka is doing the stabilizing work.

    ADL-003 · Purification, Sacrificial Discharge, and Scapegoat Logic

    S897 Legitimized enemy-designation locates fault in the target rather than in the collective.

    S899 Sacrificing the target is treated as making the community clean of fault.

    S900 After sacrificing the target, the collective constructs that suffering as a worthy victim.

    S901 The harm of the legitimized enemy is narrated as the community's repair.

    S902 After one sacrificial target is removed, legitimacy-conferring enemy-designation repeats toward another.

    S904 The underlying fault belongs to the network, not to the singled-out person.

    S903 (ADL-003-G) la-ka-no-de-su be lo-wi-fe'de-li'si lakanodesu be lowife'deli'si Purification discourse strengthens designation

    Key structural findings:

    Finding 1: scapegoat logic begins with source-relocation. ADL-003-A moves de-su from the collective to the singled-out person. That is the minimal structural operation required before purification language can become plausible.

    Finding 2: purification language converts coercion into repair. ADL-003-B/C show the sequence: once fault is localized in the target, the collective can describe itself as cleansing the network, and sacrifice can appear to clear communal fault.

    Finding 3: victim-worth can be re-used to ratify sacrifice. ADL-003-D imports vo-de-zo-li into scapegoat politics. The target's suffering is not merely inflicted; it is morally reframed as socially good.

    Finding 4: the mechanism is iterative, not curative. ADL-003-F shows the key failure mode: after sacrifice, designation repeats. The discharge does not solve the problem because the fault was never actually located in the target.

    Finding 5: the corrective diagnosis is systemic. ADL-003-H states the counterclaim directly: the underlying fault belongs to the network. That gives the batch its analytic exit from scapegoat logic.

    S912 (ADL-004-H) la-si-de si lo-wi-fe'de-li / ne wi-ra laside si lowife'deli / ne wira Record-form designation as directed power

    Key structural findings:

    Finding 1: enemy-designation becomes durable when it is codified. ADL-004-A/B move the mechanism from ideological and ritual circulation into the state-apparatus + document layer. Once designation enters wi-fe'ka-to-fe and si-ko-mu, it gains administrative persistence.

    Finding 2: records replay the category after public heat cools. ADL-004-C shows the cold stabilizer missing from earlier passes: si-de can keep re-signaling the designation even after fa-de.

    Finding 3: bureaucratic judgment makes the category portable. ADL-004-D shows how ka-to-fe on the record extends the designation from one case to another. Administration does not merely store the category; it generalizes it.

    Finding 4: exclusion becomes procedure rather than spectacle. ADL-004-E/F connect documentary + normative alignment directly to no-wi-fe and no-ne-su-li. The target can now be excluded through ordinary process, not only through renewed public fury.

    Finding 5: normalization depends on laundering norm into fact. ADL-004-G/H compress the central diagnostic: the apparatus presents a categorization rule as su-to, but what it actually wields is wi-ra through record-form. Bureaucratic neutrality is therefore itself part of the coercive effect.
