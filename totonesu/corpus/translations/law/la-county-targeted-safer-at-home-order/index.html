    LA County Targeted Safer at Home Order

    Source

    Working from the Los Angeles County Department of Public Health press release of November 27, 2020 because the underlying order text is harder to recover cleanly, while the official release still states the operative movement-and-presence logic the comparison track needs.

    Operative lines used for this municipal subtype:

    "A new Health Officer Order would be issued for three weeks that offered additional safety modifications while allowing essential and emergency workers and those securing or providing essential and permitted services to leave their homes."

    "In the new Order that goes into effect on Monday, residents are advised to stay home as much as possible and always wear a face covering over their nose and mouth when they are outside their household and around others."

    "All public and private gatherings with individuals not in your household are prohibited, except for faith based services and protests"

    "Closed non-essential businesses/activities: Playgrounds ... Cardrooms"

    "All schools and day camps remain open ... Schools and Day Camps with an outbreak (3 cases or more over 14 days) should close for 14 days."

    This batch is deliberately not another venue-entry proof rule. It isolates the other municipal COVID shape the comparison track needed: a local safer-at-home regime that governs generalized movement, gathering, masking, and opening-status through essentiality and outbreak conditions.

    It tests:

    • whether the municipal layer can govern presence and movement directly rather than only through entry gates
    • whether the real carveout is not vaccination-status but essential / permitted activity status
    • whether cross-household gathering prohibition is a distinct burden from proof-of-status exclusion
    • whether masking here tracks outside-home co-presence rather than workplace or venue-entry status
    • whether local school closure can be triggered by outbreak status even while the general order leaves some sectors open
    • whether this LA-county style is structurally distinct from NYC's venue-pass municipal model

    Sentence Analyses

    S1320 - COV-MUN-002-A: The safer-at-home order turns home-departure into the controlled default

    Notation: la-wi-fe-su ne wi-fe-ka [la-i-zo-li ka-ki-de lo-ne-pa]

    Written: lawifesu ne wifeka [laizoli kakide lonepa]

    Natural reading: The rule forbids a person from leaving the home-place.

    Notes: The press release phrases this in advisory language: residents are advised to stay home as much as possible. But the municipal shape under test is still a departure-restriction regime. Tonesu makes that control surface explicit instead of leaving it as mood-setting rhetoric around a list of downstream restrictions.

    S1321 - COV-MUN-002-B: Emergency and permitted-service roles reopen departure from home

    Notation: go {la-i-zo-li ne ka-no-de-li / la-i-zo-li ne su-ka-li}, no-wi-fe-ka [la-i-zo-li ka-ki-de lo-ne-pa]

    Written: go {laizoli ne kanodeli / laizoli ne sukali}, nowifeka [laizoli kakide lonepa]

    Natural reading: If the person is a protective worker or a service-provider, it is permitted for the person to leave the home-place.

    Notes: The official text is more exact: essential and emergency workers, plus those securing or providing essential and permitted services. The compression keeps the structural point. Departure is not universally banned and then casually excused. It is reopened by role-category and permitted-service status.

    S1322 - COV-MUN-002-C: Cross-household gathering is prohibited

    Notation: go {la-li-pu no ne lo-ne-pa}, la-wi-fe-su ne wi-fe-ka lo-o-ka

    Written: go {lalipu no ne lonepa}, lawifesu ne wifeka looka

    Natural reading: If the persons are not of one home-place, the rule forbids their acting together.

    Notes: This is the direct rendering of the gathering ban. o-ka is intentionally broad here: the order reaches both public and private gatherings and turns household-boundary status into the decisive limit. That is different from NYC's proof-gated venue threshold and from OSHA's workplace-presence calibration.

    S1323 - COV-MUN-002-D: Outside-home co-presence triggers face covering

    Notation: go {la-i-zo-li no ne lo-ne-pa / la-yu ne lo-ko-pa}, la-i-zo-li ne ne-fe lo-vo-mu ne lo-pa-zo-tu

    Written: go {laizoli no ne lonepa / layu ne lokopa}, laizoli ne nefe lovomu ne lopazotu

    Natural reading: If the person is not at the home-place and other persons are present, the person must have a face covering.

    Notes: The press release says residents must wear a face covering when outside their household and around others. This masking burden therefore rides on outside-home co-presence, not on vaccination status, and not only on employer or venue surfaces.

    S1324 - COV-MUN-002-E: School outbreak status triggers closure

    Notation: go {la-to-ki-ne-su si [de-su be]}, wi-fe-ka [la-i-zo-li ki lo-to-ki-ne-su]

    Written: go {latokinesu si [desu be]}, wifeka [laizoli ki lotokinesu]

    Natural reading: If the educational institution shows growing outbreak-status, persons may not enter the educational institution.

    Notes: The source text is more precise than this compression: outbreak means three cases or more over fourteen days, and the closure period is fourteen days. The structural point is already visible. The local order does not simply announce a universal home lock. It keeps schools open conditionally and then shuts them when outbreak status crosses a threshold.

    Entry Tonesu Written Claim Key feature

    S1324 go {la-to-ki-ne-su si [de-su be]}, wi-fe-ka [la-i-zo-li ki lo-to-ki-ne-su] go {latokinesu si [desu be]}, wifeka [laizoli ki lotokinesu] outbreak status closes schools threshold-triggered local closure

    Key findings:

    1. This municipal layer is not an entry-pass regime. It is a safer-at-home structure that governs departure, gathering, and co-presence directly.
    2. The carveout logic runs through role and service category, not through vaccination proof. The essential/non-essential distinction is the controlling geometry.
    3. Household boundary is load-bearing. Cross-household gathering becomes the relevant prohibited relation even when no venue operator is mediating entry.
    4. Masking here is generalized co-presence governance. It is neither OSHA's workplace condition nor NYC's venue threshold.
    5. Local school closure shows that the order is not a single binary lockdown. It is a layered local-management regime that leaves some sectors conditionally open and then closes them by outbreak trigger.
    6. COV-MUN-002 therefore confirms the earlier hypothesis from COV-MUN-001: LA-style "non-essential persons" logic is a separate municipal subtype and should not be collapsed into the NYC venue-pass batch.
