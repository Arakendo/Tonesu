# Translation Test: Mao Zedong, *Quotations from Chairman Mao Tse-tung* ("The Little Red Book") and Selected Writings

## Source: Mao Zedong, various writings and speeches, compiled in *Quotations from Chairman Mao Tse-tung* (1964); additional sources noted per entry
## Original language: Chinese (Mandarin)
## Reference: Standard English translations of the canonical quoted forms
## Status: Draft — MAO-001 first pass (S801–S812)

---

## Purpose

The Mao batch is a **power, class, and dialectics vocabulary stress test** — adjacent to ALI-001 (adversarial mechanics) and SLZ-001/002 (mutual knowledge and epistemic fraud) but in a distinct register: the claims are collectivist, historical, and explicitly dialectical. The vocabulary pressure falls on:

1. **`wi-ra` from `ka-mu-ra`** — Rule 1: power originates in the weapon. The `go/be` causal frame asserts an origin claim about what generates `wi-ra`. First explicit `go {instrument}, wi-ra be` structure.
2. **Appearance/reality via `/`** — Rule 2 (paper tigers): the same entity is simultaneously a signal of power and the absence of power. This is the fourth distinct `/` function in corpus: (1) constitutive pair (ALI S777), (2) negated choice (JBP S792), (3) co-presence (JBP S800), (4) appearance/reality divergence (MAO S802).
3. **`ge-ki-ka-li-su` = revolution** — Rule 3: fundamental alteration of governance. Compositional 4-root compound; marks the first use of `ge-ki` (W187, fundamental alteration) applied to `ka-li-su` (W147, governance).
4. **`pu-` as compound quantity prefix** — Rule 5 (hundred flowers): `pu-zo` = many organisms / many flowers; `pu-to-su` = many knowledge-systems / many schools. First systematic use of `pu` (quantity/plurality primitive) as a compound-initial quantifier on a noun compound.
5. **`ke,` inside `/` (second instance)** — Rule 7 (war through war): the paradox of means and end. `la-mi wi-no ka-ra / ke, go {ka-ra}, du {no-ka-ra}` = we don't want war / pivot: from war, no-war. Cross-register confirmation (first: JBP ethics S796; now: political-military).
6. **`go-si` as pure simile adverbial** — Rule 9 (fish in sea): first use of `go-si` as a manner-adverbial simile clause (`ki ko {o-li} go-si zo-ki ki ko {pa-ki'ma}`). Distinct from AOW-001's predicate-property use (`ne go-si [X]`).
7. **Dialectical co-presence** — Rule 12 (chaos + excellent): the final sentence puts `wi-no-su` (chaos) and `vo-be` (growing value) in `/` parallel as simultaneously-true conditions. `vo-be` cross-domain application: from JBP ethical/developmental register (S795) to Maoist political-historical register.

Secondary tests: four-clause `;` imperative chain (S808); `go {no-[X]}, no [Y]` negative-causal frame (S811); `ru-fe,` in exclusive-historical-agency role (S806, cf. EXC-001 and DOI-001).

## MAO-001 — Twelve Quotations (S801–S812)

### S801 — MAO-001-A

**Source:** "Political power grows out of the barrel of a gun." — *Problems of War and Strategy* (1938); LRB Chapter 5.

**Notation:** `go {ka-mu-ra},  wi-ra  be`

**Written:** `go {kamura}, wira be`

**Gloss:** from [force-instrument/weapon] → directed-power grows

**Natural reading:** Directed authority originates in and grows from the weapon.

**Notes:** `ka-mu-ra` = `ka-mu` (W047, tool/action-instrument) + `ra` (force/energy) = force-instrument = weapon. The `ra` suffix specifies that this particular `ka-mu` is a *force*-instrument, not merely any tool. `go {ka-mu-ra}` = the causal origin of what follows. `wi-ra be` = directed-power increases/grows. First explicit `go {instrument}, wi-ra be` structure in corpus: the gun is not the power but the origin from which power grows. Mao's claim is not merely that guns are useful but that `wi-ra` has a specific ontological origin in force-instruments.

---

### S802 — MAO-001-B

**Source:** "All reactionaries are paper tigers." — LRB Chapter 6; originally from *Talk with the American Correspondent Anna Louise Strong* (1946).

**Notation:** `[ka-no-be-li]  ne  si [wi-ra]  /  la-ze  ne  no-wi-ra`

**Written:** `[kanobell] ne si [wira] / laze ne nowira`

**Gloss:** [deliberate-growth-preventers] are signal-of-power / they are no-power

**Natural reading:** Reactionaries present themselves as powerful; in reality they have no directed power.

**Notes:** `ka-no-be-li` = `ka` (deliberate action) + `no` (negation) + `be` (growth) + `li` (person) = a person who deliberately acts to prevent growth/progress = reactionary. This is Mao's ideological definition encoded compositionally. Left arm: `ne si [wi-ra]` = the property of being a power-signal = appearance. Right arm: `la-ze ne no-wi-ra` = they are [the absence of directed power] = reality. The `/` marks the fourth distinct function in corpus: appearance/reality divergence. The same entity simultaneously carries the property `si [wi-ra]` (appears powerful) and `no-wi-ra` (lacks power). This is distinct from constitutive pair (ALI), negated choice (JBP Rule 4), and co-presence (JBP Rule 12).

### S803 — MAO-001-C

**Source:** "A revolution is not a dinner party, or writing an essay, or painting a picture, or doing embroidery... A revolution is an insurrection, an act of violence by which one class overthrows another." — *Report on an Investigation of the Peasant Movement in Hunan* (1927); spirit preserved in LRB.

**Notation:** `ge-ki-ka-li-su  ne  no  fa-vo  /  ne  ka-ra`

**Written:** `gekikalisu ne no favo / ne kara`

**Gloss:** fundamental-alteration-governance is not pleasant / is combat

**Natural reading:** Revolution is not a pleasant event — it is an act of martial force.

**Notes:** `ge-ki-ka-li-su` = `ge-ki` (W187, fundamental alteration of properties / transmutation) + `ka-li-su` (W147, governance/dominion) = fundamental alteration of governance = revolution; first use. Parse: `ge-ki` modifies `[ka-li-su]` as a whole. `ne no fa-vo` = has the property of not-pleasant = is not enjoyable. `ne ka-ra` = has the property of combat/warfare (W222). The `/` pairs the two halves of Mao's claim: what revolution is not / what revolution is. This is the constitutive-pair function of `/` (ALI S777 pattern) but with a negated left arm — first combination of `no` and `/` in this position.

### S804 — MAO-001-D

**Source:** "Serve the People." — Title of speech (1944); pervasive throughout LRB.

**Notation:** `ka  wi-vo  lo-o-li`

**Written:** `ka wivo loooli`

**Gloss:** act with beneficent-will toward collective-persons

**Natural reading:** Act for the benefit of the collective people.

**Notes:** The shortest imperative in this batch and among the simplest in the corpus. `ka` = deliberate action (imperative: act). `wi-vo` (W190) = beneficent will / will toward good. `lo-o-li` = patient marker + collective-persons = toward the collective. The `o-` scope prefix on `li` marks this as the aggregate collective, not any individual. Mao's formula in Tonesu is: act-beneficently toward the aggregate people. The sentence has no explicit agent (`la-` phrase) — agentless imperative as established in SNG-002 and MRX-001 patterns.

### S805 — MAO-001-E

**Source:** "Let a hundred flowers bloom; let a hundred schools of thought contend." — *On the Correct Handling of Contradictions Among the People* (1957); associated with LRB.

**Notation:** `be  pu-zo  ;  zi-ka  pu-to-su`

**Written:** `be puzo ; zika putosu`

**Gloss:** grow many-organisms ; intentional-exchange among many-knowledge-systems

**Natural reading:** Let many organisms flourish; let many organized schools of thought engage one another.

**Notes:** `pu-zo` = `pu` (quantity/plurality, primitive) + `zo` (living organism) = many living things/plants/flowers. First use of `pu` as a compound-initial quantity prefix on a noun compound. `be pu-zo` = many organisms grow = agentless growth-clause (PAV-001 emergence pattern: `lo-P be`). `zi-ka` (W105) = intentional exchange event = contend/engage. `pu-to-su` = many + `to-su` (W030, organized knowledge) = many schools of thought. `zi-ka pu-to-su` = many knowledge-systems engage in intentional exchange with each other = let them contend. The `;` marks these as ordered but non-causally-entailed paired mandates.

### S806 — MAO-001-F

**Source:** "The people, and the people alone, are the motive force in the making of world history." — *On Coalition Government* (1945); LRB Chapter 15 area.

**Notation:** `ru-fe,  la-o-li  ka-be  lo-pa-zo-li-ti-de`

**Written:** `rufe, laoli kabe lopazolitide`

**Gloss:** solely: the collective persons make [the world's past / world-history]

**Natural reading:** The collective people alone are the agents who produce world history.

**Notes:** `ru-fe,` = exclusive scope particle (G031, clause-initial) = "solely / and only." `la-o-li` = collective-persons as the `la-`-marked agent. `ka-be` = deliberate growth/construction = make/produce. `lo-pa-zo-li-ti-de` = patient: `pa-zo-li` (W135, the inhabited world of human persons) + `ti-de` (W041, past time) = world-past = world history; first use. `ru-fe,` here asserts exclusive historical agency: it is the collective people and nothing/no one else that is the productive force of world history. Third use of `ru-fe,` in an explicit historical-agency role (cf. EXC-001, DOI-001).

### S807 — MAO-001-G

**Source:** "War can only be abolished through war, and in order to get rid of the gun it is necessary to take up the gun." — *Problems of War and Strategy* (1938); LRB Chapter 5.

**Notation:** `la-mi  wi-no  ka-ra  /  ke,  go {ka-ra},  du {no-ka-ra}`

**Written:** `lami wino kara / ke, go {kara}, du {nokara}`

**Gloss:** I am-unwilling [toward] war / pivot: from [war] → result [no-war]

**Natural reading:** We do not want war; but from war and only from war does the abolition of war come.

**Notes:** Left arm: `la-mi wi-no ka-ra` = I am unwilling toward combat/war (W230, W222). `ke,` = silent pivot: the refusal-of-war claim is contextually present (and was stated in the left arm); `ke,` advances without re-performing it. Right arm: `go {ka-ra}, du {no-ka-ra}` = from [war] the result is [no-war]. `no-ka-ra` = negation of combat = abolition/absence of war. The paradox: the means (war) is the negation of the end (no-war), yet the causal chain `go/du` asserts the mechanism is real. This is the second use of `ke,` inside a `/` construction (first: JBP S796); confirmed cross-register (ethical → political-military).

### S808 — MAO-001-H

**Source:** "Be resolute, fear no sacrifice, surmount every difficulty, to win victory." — LRB Chapter 30.

**Notation:** `wi-ra  ;  no  fa-ra  lo-de-zo  ;  ka  lo-a-de  ;  wi-du`

**Written:** `wira ; no fara lodeze ; ka loade ; widu`

**Gloss:** directed-will ; no fear of death ; act toward all-difficulty ; victory

**Natural reading:** Direct your will; do not fear death; act against every obstacle; achieve victory.

**Notes:** Four `;`-chained imperative elements — matches ALI-001 S788 as the longest tactical sequence in corpus. Each element is agentless:
- `wi-ra` = directed will = be resolute
- `no fa-ra lo-de-zo` = no fear (`fa-ra`, W154) of death (`de-zo`, W178)  = fear no sacrifice
- `ka lo-a-de` = act toward universal-difficulty: `a-` (universal scope prefix) + `de` (decay/decrease) = all obstacles/difficulties; `lo-a-de` = patient of all-difficulty = "every obstacle"; first use of `a-de` compound
- `wi-du` = victory (W175) = to win

The `;` is correct: the four elements are ordered (left-to-right), but the mechanism connecting resolve → fearlessness → action → victory is not formally asserted. Mao presents them as the four conditions of winning, not a causal chain.

### S809 — MAO-001-I

**Source:** "The guerrilla must move amongst the people as a fish swims in the sea." — attributed to Mao; from guerrilla warfare writings.

**Notation:** `se-de-li  ne-fe  ki  ko {o-li}  go-si  zo-ki  ki  ko {pa-ki'ma}`

**Written:** `sedeli nefe ki ko {oli} gosi zoki ki ko {pakima}`

**Gloss:** stealth-agent requires moving-within [collective] like [motion-organism moves-within liquid-place]

**Natural reading:** The guerrilla must move embedded within the people as a fish moves through the sea.

**Notes:** `se-de-li` (W208) = rogue/stealth agent = guerrilla (the covert-embedding function; in context of `ne-fe ki ko {o-li}` the martial meaning is unambiguous). `ne-fe` (W042) = structural requirement. `ki ko {o-li}` = motion within [collective] = move among the people (containment `ko` + collective `o-li`). `go-si` = in-the-manner-of = like; first use as a pure simile/manner adverbial. Previously `go-si` appeared in AOW-001 as predicate-property (`ne go-si [X]` = has the property of originating from); here it introduces a manner clause: `go-si zo-ki ki ko {pa-ki'ma}` = in the manner that a motion-organism moves within a liquid-place. `zo-ki` = motion-organism = fish-proxy (living thing defined by motion through liquid in context). `pa-ki'ma` = `pa` (place) + `ki'ma` (W114, liquid) = liquid-place = sea/body of water; first use.

### S810 — MAO-001-J

**Source:** "Practice is the sole criterion for judging truth." — *On Practice* (1937); LRB spirit.

**Notation:** `ru-fe,  ka  to-fe  lo-to-ne-su`

**Written:** `rufe, ka tofe lotoonesu`

**Gloss:** solely: action is the epistemic-criterion of truth

**Natural reading:** Deliberate practice alone is the test that determines what is true.

**Notes:** `ru-fe,` = exclusively (clause-initial). `ka` = deliberate action / practice (as noun in predicate position: practice / the act). `to-fe` (W028) = epistemic boundary / the line between knowledge states = criterion / the determining threshold. `lo-to-ne-su` = of truth (W000). `ru-fe, ka to-fe lo-to-ne-su` = solely, action is the epistemic boundary of truth. Mao's empiricist claim: no abstract reasoning or received doctrine (`to-su`) can substitute for the test of practice. Compare JBP S797 (epistemic humility as deliberate act) — both are about the limits of unapplied knowledge, but the mechanisms are different: JBP attributes knowledge to others; Mao tests knowledge through action.

### S811 — MAO-001-K

**Source:** "No investigation, no right to speak." — *Oppose Book Worship* (1930); LRB Chapter 1 area.

**Notation:** `go {no-se-ka},  no  wi-fe  lo-si-ki`

**Written:** `go {noseka}, no wife losiki`

**Gloss:** from [no-investigation] → no right to signal

**Natural reading:** Without investigation, you have no right to speak.

**Notes:** `no-se-ka` = negation + `se-ka` (W034, examination/deliberate inspection) = no investigation. The `go {}` causal frame makes this a structural claim: the absence of investigation is the *cause* of the absence of speaking-rights — not merely correlated. `wi-fe` (W100) = rule/right/permission. `lo-si-ki` = patient of signal-transmission = the right to signal/speak. `no wi-fe lo-si-ki` = no right to speak. First `go {no-[X]}, no [Y]` negative-causal frame: the negated condition in the premise yields a negated permission in the result. Mao's epistemological authority-claim: the right to make political claims is grounded in and *caused* by prior investigation. No investigation → no grounds → no right.

### S812 — MAO-001-L

**Source:** "There is great disorder under heaven — the situation is excellent." — attributed to Mao Zedong, Cultural Revolution era (c. 1966–1967); associated with Maoist dialectical analysis; note: not in the canonical LRB text.

**Notation:** `a-ne-su  ne  wi-no-su  /  su-ti  ne  vo-be`

**Written:** `anesu ne winosu / suti ne vobe`

**Gloss:** the universal-network is chaotic / the current-state is growing-value

**Natural reading:** The whole situation is in chaos; the situation is excellent.

**Notes:** `a-ne-su` = `a-` (universal/abstract scope prefix) + `ne-su` (W031, network/society) = the universal network = everything / the whole situation under heaven. `wi-no-su` (W189) = chaotic / will unbound by structure. `su-ti` (W101) = current state / instantaneous configuration. `vo-be` = growing-value = excellent / opportunity-rich (first used JBP-001 S795 in ethical register; now political register). The `/` marks co-presence: both predicates are simultaneously true of the same situation. This is not a choice (JBP Rule 4), not a constitutive pair (ALI Rule 1), not appearance/reality (MAO S802). It is the Maoist dialectical move: chaos and opportunity are not contradictory but co-present. The claim that `su-ti ne vo-be` requires `a-ne-su ne wi-no-su` to be true at the same time — the worse the external chaos, the better the revolutionary conditions. The `/` holds them together without asserting why.

**New W-entries:** none (all vocabulary compositional from registered forms).

**Compositional first uses:**
- `ka-mu-ra` = weapon / force-instrument (S801): `ka-mu` W047 + `ra`
- `ka-no-be-li` = reactionary / deliberate-growth-preventer (S802): `ka` + `no` + `be` + `li`
- `ge-ki-ka-li-su` = revolution / fundamental-alteration-governance (S803): W187 + W147
- `pu-zo` = many organisms / many plants (S805): `pu` + `zo`
- `pu-to-su` = many knowledge-systems / many schools of thought (S805): `pu` + W030
- `pa-zo-li-ti-de` = world-history / the world's past (S806): W135 + W041
- `a-de` = universal difficulty / all obstacles (S808): `a-` + `de`
- `pa-ki'ma` = sea / liquid-place / body of water (S809): `pa` + W114
- `zo-ki` = motion-organism / fish-proxy (S809): `zo` + `ki`

**Key structural findings:**

1. **Fourth distinct `/` function: appearance/reality divergence** (S802). Previous `/` functions: (1) constitutive pair — ALI S777; (2) negated choice — JBP S792; (3) co-presence — JBP S800; (4) now: appearance/reality — the same entity simultaneously is `si [wi-ra]` (appears powerful) and `no-wi-ra` (is not). The left arm is the phenomenal description; the right arm is the noumenal reality.

2. **`ge-ki-ka-li-su` = revolution** (S803). `ge-ki` (W187, fundamental alteration) + `ka-li-su` (W147, governance) = fundamental alteration of governance. The compound encodes Mao's structural definition: revolution is not reform or gradual change but qualitative transmutation of the governance structure. Candidate for W-registration if further attestations accumulate.

3. **`pu-` as compound quantity prefix** (S805). First systematic use of `pu` (quantity/plurality primitive) as compound-initial on a noun compound: `pu-zo` = many organisms, `pu-to-su` = many knowledge-systems. `pu` was previously used bare or in quantity discussions; here it functions as a multiplicity modifier that scales up any noun compound to plural/many.

4. **`ke,` inside `/` confirmed cross-register** (S807). Second instance (first: JBP S796, ethical register). Pattern: `[stated position] / ke, [structural claim that advances from the stated position without re-performing it]`. The `/` holds the two clauses as formally paired; `ke,` pivots within the right arm. The structure is now confirmed in both ethical and political-military registers.

5. **`go-si` as pure simile adverbial** (S809). First use of `go-si` as a manner-adverbial clause introducing a simile. AOW-001 established `ne go-si [X]` as a predicate-property construction ("has the property originating from X"). Here: `ki ko {o-li} go-si zo-ki ki ko {pa-ki'ma}` = "moves within the collective in the manner that a motion-organism moves within liquid-place." `go-si` now has two confirmed functions: (a) predicate-property and (b) manner/simile adverbial.

6. **`go {no-[X]}, no [Y]` negative-causal frame** (S811). First instance. The negated condition in the premise (`no-se-ka` = no investigation) causes the negated permission in the result (`no wi-fe lo-si-ki` = no right to speak). This is a productive pattern: absence of prior condition → absence of derived right. Structure mirrors `go {X}, Y` but with double negation across premise and result.

7. **`vo-be` cross-domain political application** (S812). First used JBP-001 S795 in the ethical/developmental register (meaningful = what increases in value over time). Here in the political-historical register: `su-ti ne vo-be` = the current state has growing value = the situation is excellent. Same compound, new domain. The Maoist claim: the condition for `su-ti ne vo-be` is `a-ne-su ne wi-no-su` — chaos is the ground of opportunity.

8. **`/` as dialectical co-presence vs. antithetical contrast** (S812). S803 used `/` as negated-left/positive-right (not X / is Y — antithetical). S812 uses `/` as co-presence (X and Y are both true simultaneously — dialectical). Both have the surface form `A / B` but with different logical relationships between the arms. The distinction is now documented.
