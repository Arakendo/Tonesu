## WFE-002 — Reflexive Application: Self-Licensing Circularity (S873–S880)

**Batch:** WFE-002  
**Source:** Structural extension of WFE-001. Tests whether `wife'atofeka` can be predicated of its own operating mechanism — the self-referential form.  
**Entries:** S873–S880 · **New vocabulary:** none (all compositional)

**Key tests:**
- `la-X ne go-si X` — self-referential instrumental-property predication (X operates by means of X); first use
- `no-go-si [X]` — negated instrumental; first use
- `go {premise}, result` conditional with `wife'atofeka ne go-si wife'atofeka` as clausal object of `to`
- `to-su lo-wife'atofeka` — extending the `to-su lo-[NP]` relational knowledge pattern from AOW-001

**Central claim:** `wife'atofeka` is not merely a property a system can have — it is self-licensing. The entitlement-structure (`wi-fe`) that licenses bounded knowing is itself an exercise of bounded knowing. This makes `wife'atofeka` structurally distinct from propaganda (`ka-si-de`), which requires an external truth-ground and dissolves under exposure. `wife'atofeka` has no such external dependency: the legitimating frame and the thing being legitimated are the same structure.

---

### S873 — WFE-002-A

**Source:** "Legitimized epistemic closure licenses itself."

**Tonesu:** `la-wife'atofeka  ne  go-si  wife'atofeka`

**Written:** `lawife'atofeka ne gosi wife'atofeka`

**Gloss:** [legitimized-epistemic-closure] IS by-means-of [legitimized-epistemic-closure]

**Natural reading:** Epistemic closure legitimizes itself.

**Notes:** First use of `la-X ne go-si X` — self-referential instrumental-property predication. Extends the `ne go-si [X]` pattern from AOW-001 (S702: `a-ka-ra ne go-si ka-si-de` — all warfare IS by means of deception). There, `go-si` indexed an external mechanism (deception is the means of warfare). Here, the mechanism IS the same thing as the subject: `wife'atofeka ne go-si wife'atofeka`. The entitlement-frame (`wi-fe`) that licenses bounded knowing is itself an exercise of bounded knowing. Both sides of `ne go-si` are the same compound — the circularity is structurally visible in the sentence shape.

---

### S874 — WFE-002-B

**Source:** "Propaganda does not license itself."

**Tonesu:** `la-ka-si-de  no  ne  go-si  ka-si-de`

**Written:** `lakaside no ne gosi kaside`

**Gloss:** [propaganda] NOT IS by-means-of [propaganda]

**Natural reading:** Propaganda does not operate by means of propaganda.

**Notes:** Minimal pair with S873. Propaganda (`ka-si-de`, W224) cannot self-seal: it requires an external truth to suppress or distort. Once exposed, it dissolves (S868 contrasts: knowing `wife'atofeka` does not dissolve it). Structural asymmetry: propaganda is parasitic on an external truth-ground; `wife'atofeka` has no such dependency. `no ne go-si` = "is not by means of" — `no` negates the predicate `ne go-si ka-si-de` as a block. The negated form also contrasts with the positive self-licensing of S873, making S873 and S874 a structural minimal pair distinguished only by `no`.

---

### S875 — WFE-002-C

**Source:** "This distinction, stated as a single canonical parallel."

**Tonesu:** `la-wife'atofeka  ne  go-si  wife'atofeka  /  la-ka-si-de  no  ne  go-si  ka-si-de`

**Written:** `lawife'atofeka ne gosi wife'atofeka / lakaside no ne gosi kaside`

**Gloss:** [epistemic-closure] IS by-means-of [epistemic-closure] / [propaganda] NOT IS by-means-of [propaganda]

**Natural reading:** Epistemic closure is self-licensing / propaganda is not self-licensing.

**Notes:** Full `/` parallel of S873 and S874 as a canonical diagnostic sentence. The two clauses are structurally paired — the same `la-X ne go-si X` frame, with `no` as the only difference. If one sentence is needed to explain why `wife'atofeka` ≠ `ka-si-de`, this is it. The `/` marker pairs the two clauses as a structural contrast, not an antithesis: both descriptions are true — they are two different structural types, distinguished by whether the operating mechanism is self-referential or externally grounded.

---

### S876 — WFE-002-D

**Source:** "Even knowing the mechanism doesn't get you out."

**Tonesu:** `go {la-zo-li  to [wife'atofeka  ne  go-si  wife'atofeka]},  la-zo-li  ne  wife'atofeka`

**Written:** `go {lazoli to [wife'atofeka ne gosi wife'atofeka]}, lazoli ne wife'atofeka`

**Gloss:** Given [a-person knows [epistemic-closure IS by-means-of epistemic-closure]], that-person IS epistemic-closure

**Natural reading:** Given a person knows that epistemic closure is self-licensing, that person is still inside epistemic closure.

**Notes:** Extends S868 (knowing propaganda dissolves it; knowing `wife'atofeka` does not) to the meta-level: knowing the self-licensing mechanism doesn't dissolve it either. The `go {premise}, result` conditional frame (well-attested: AOW-001, PRF-001) makes the implication explicit. The premise is `la-zo-li to [wife'atofeka ne go-si wife'atofeka]` — a person knows the self-referential fact. The result is `la-zo-li ne wife'atofeka` — that same person (`la-zo-li` recovered across the frame) is inside the closure. **First use of `wife'atofeka ne go-si wife'atofeka` as a clausal object of `to`.** The three levels of failure now attested: (1) object-level exposure — S868; (2) meta-awareness of non-dissolution — S868; (3) meta-awareness of self-licensing — S876. All three leave the knower inside.

---

### S877 — WFE-002-E

**Source:** "Omniscience knows the structure and is not inside it."

**Tonesu:** `la-to-no-fe  to [wife'atofeka  ne  go-si  wife'atofeka]  /  no  ne  wife'atofeka`

**Written:** `latonofe to [wife'atofeka ne gosi wife'atofeka] / no ne wife'atofeka`

**Gloss:** [omniscience] knows [epistemic-closure IS by-means-of epistemic-closure] / NOT IS epistemic-closure

**Natural reading:** Omniscience knows that epistemic closure is self-licensing / and is not inside epistemic closure.

**Notes:** Extends S870 (`la-to-no-fe ne no-wife'atofeka` — omniscience is the negation of epistemic closure) with the self-licensing form as the clausal object. The `/` pairs two facts about omniscience: (1) it can know the self-licensing structure (`to [clause]`); (2) it is not subject to it (`no ne wife'atofeka`). Contrasts with S876: where the finite knower who knows the self-licensing fact is still inside, omniscience knows the same fact from outside. The difference: for omniscience, there is no `fe` (knowledge boundary) — the `a-to-fe-ka` inside `wife'atofeka` cannot apply to an unbounded knower. **Second use of `wife'atofeka ne go-si wife'atofeka` as clausal object of `to`.** Argument-drop: `no ne wife'atofeka` is agentless — subject recovered from the left clause agent (`la-to-no-fe`), cross-batch confirmation of argument-drop (first attested MRX-001 S719).

---

### S878 — WFE-002-F

**Source:** "Natality exits structurally, not epistemically."

**Tonesu:** `la-be-ki  ne  no-go-si  wife'atofeka`

**Written:** `labeki ne nogosi wife'atofeka`

**Gloss:** [natality] IS not-by-means-of [epistemic-closure]

**Natural reading:** Natality does not operate through epistemic closure.

**Notes:** The structural escape condition. Where S877 shows omniscience escaping epistemically (by having no boundary at all), S878 shows natality escaping structurally. `no-go-si wife'atofeka` = "not-by-means-of epistemic closure" — the instrumental is negated, following the `no-[compound]` pattern (`no-wi-fe-ka`, `no-ne-zo-li`, `no-go-si`). **First use of `no-go-si [compound]` as a negated instrumental.** Natality (`be-ki`, W244) is the capacity for new beginning — and the new beginning is constitutively outside any prior closure: it is not licensed by the existing legitimizing structure, because it is by definition structurally novel. The exit from self-licensing closure is not epistemic (knowing differently — that fails, S876) but structural (beginning newly). First use of `no-go-si`.

---

### S879 — WFE-002-G

**Source:** "The academic study of epistemic closure is itself inside it."

**Tonesu:** `la-to-su  lo-wife'atofeka  ne  wife'atofeka`

**Written:** `latosu lowife'atofeka ne wife'atofeka`

**Gloss:** [structured-knowledge-of-epistemic-closure] IS epistemic-closure

**Natural reading:** The structured analysis of epistemic closure is itself epistemic closure.

**Notes:** Institutional form of S876. `to-su lo-wife'atofeka` = "structured knowledge about epistemic closure" — the academic or scholarly discourse that systematically analyzes the condition. Extends the `to-su lo-[NP]` relational knowledge pattern from AOW-001 (`to-su lo-de-li` = knowledge of the adversary; `to-su lo-mi` = knowledge of oneself). **First use of `to-su lo-[W-entry compound]` where the patient compound is itself a knowledge-bounding form.** The claim: the body of organized critical analysis studying `wife'atofeka` is shaped by `wife'atofeka`. The scholarly apparatus that names and studies epistemic closure is itself inside what it studies. This is the institutional complement to the individual case (S876): individuals who know the mechanism are inside; institutions that study the mechanism are also inside.

---

### S880 — WFE-002-H

**Source:** "Only natality exits — the exclusive claim."

**Tonesu:** `ru-fe,  la-be-ki  ne  no-go-si  wife'atofeka`

**Written:** `rufe, labeki ne nogosi wife'atofeka`

**Gloss:** ONLY [natality] IS not-by-means-of [epistemic-closure]

**Natural reading:** Only natality does not operate through epistemic closure.

**Notes:** `ru-fe,` + S878 = the exclusive claim. Natality is not just AN exit from self-licensing closure — it is the ONLY exit available to finite beings. `ru-fe,` (G031, clause-initial exclusive scope particle) scopes over the full predicate `la-be-ki ne no-go-si wife'atofeka`. Contrasts with S877 (omniscience also exits, but is not finite). The `ru-fe,` exclusivity claim thus applies within the domain of finite agents: for all finite agents, `be-ki` is the sole mechanism that operates `no-go-si wife'atofeka`. All finite epistemic moves — critical analysis (S879), meta-awareness (S876), and object-level exposure (S868) — remain inside the closure. Only the structural novelty of new beginning (`be-ki`) is constitutively outside.

## ADL-001 — Adversarial Designation and Action-Space Activation (S881–S888)

**Batch:** ADL-001  
**Source:** Structural test battery derived from the distinction between descriptive adversary-identification and legitimacy-transforming designation. Tests whether adversarial labeling remains truth-tracking (`de-li`) or redraws the permissible-action boundary (`ka-fe`) toward a target.  
**Entries:** S881–S888 · **New vocabulary:** none (all compositional)

**Key tests:**
- `de-li'si` — adversary-designation as a label-act distinct from the target category itself; first use
- `wi-fe'de-li` — legitimacy-framed adversary / morally authorized enemy; first use
- `ki lo-ka-fe` — changing the action-boundary as the diagnostic of activation rather than mere description
- `go {premise}, result` linking designation to `wi-ra`, `wi-ra-ki`, and `su-li` behavior under pressure
- `wife'atofeka` as the closure condition that stabilizes enemy-designation against contradiction exposure

**Central claim:** An adversarial label becomes structurally distinct when it stops merely classifying a target and begins transforming the permissible action-space toward that target. In Tonesu terms, the decisive shift is not from true label to false label, but from `de-li` (relational adversary) to a designation that acquires `wi-fe` and thereby changes `ka-fe`. At that point, the label is no longer only descriptive; it becomes a coercive-legitimacy activator.

---

### S881 — ADL-001-A

**Source:** "Adversary-designation by itself does not change what may be done."

**Tonesu:** `la-de-li'si  no  ki  lo-ka-fe`

**Written:** `ladeli'si no ki lokafe`

**Gloss:** [adversary-designation] NOT changes [action-boundary]

**Natural reading:** Adversary-designation does not by itself change the action-boundary.

**Notes:** Baseline descriptive case. `de-li'si` = `[de-li]'[si]` = adversary-designation: the signal or label that marks a target as adversarial. The juncture is required: without `'`, `de-li-si` right-branches as `de` + `[li-si]` = decrease + person-signal, wrong reading. `ka-fe` (S858) = deliberate-action-boundary = the limit of what may be done. The sentence defines the null case: descriptive classification alone does not yet alter permissibility. **First use of `de-li'si`.**

---

### S882 — ADL-001-B

**Source:** "Once adversary-designation is treated as legitimate, it changes what may be done."

**Tonesu:** `la-de-li'si  ne  wi-fe ;  la-de-li'si  ki  lo-ka-fe`

**Written:** `ladeli'si ne wife ; ladeli'si ki lokafe`

**Gloss:** [adversary-designation] IS legitimacy/right ; [adversary-designation] changes [action-boundary]

**Natural reading:** When adversary-designation is granted legitimacy, it changes the action-boundary.

**Notes:** The activation threshold. `ne wi-fe` does not mean the designation is factually correct; it means the designation is treated as normatively rightful or authorized. The second clause gives the diagnostic consequence: the label now changes `ka-fe`. This is the structural distinction the batch is testing: classification versus action-space transformation.

---

### S883 — ADL-001-C

**Source:** "Ideology can identify an adversary, or it can construct a legitimized enemy."

**Tonesu:** `la-to-su  si  lo-de-li  /  la-to-su  si  lo-wi-fe'de-li`

**Written:** `latosu si lodeli / latosu si lowife'deli`

**Gloss:** [ideology] designates [adversary] / [ideology] designates [legitimized-adversary]

**Natural reading:** Ideology designates an adversary / ideology designates a legitimized enemy.

**Notes:** Canonical minimal pair. Left clause: descriptive adversarial classification (`de-li`). Right clause: `wi-fe'de-li` = `[wi-fe]'[de-li]` = legitimacy-framed adversary = enemyhood presented as rightful. The juncture is required so `wi-fe` scopes over the whole adversary category rather than right-branching internally. This is the cleanest corpus distinction between truth-tracking enemy-identification and coercive moral recategorization. **First use of `wi-fe'de-li`.**

---

### S884 — ADL-001-D

**Source:** "When ideology designates a legitimized enemy, it redraws the action-boundary."

**Tonesu:** `go {la-to-su  si  lo-wi-fe'de-li},  la-to-su  ki  lo-ka-fe`

**Written:** `go {latosu si lowife'deli}, latosu ki lokafe`

**Gloss:** because [ideology designates legitimized-adversary], ideology changes [action-boundary]

**Natural reading:** Once ideology designates a legitimized enemy, it redraws what may be done.

**Notes:** Explicit causal link from designation to permissibility shift. This is the bridge missing in the existing Arendt sentence S856 (`to-su si lo-de-li / wi-ra-ki ka-de lo-de-li`): ADL-001-D names the middle operator. The label does not merely supply a target; it transforms the permissible field around the target.

**Key structural findings:**

**Finding 1: designation and target-category separate cleanly.** `de-li` names the adversarial target-role; `de-li'si` names the act of designation. This lets the corpus distinguish classifying an adversary from using that classification politically.

**Finding 2: the activation threshold is `wi-fe` + `ka-fe`.** ADL-001-B/D show the decisive move: once designation acquires legitimacy (`ne wi-fe` or `wi-fe'X`), it changes the action-boundary (`ki lo-ka-fe`). That is the operational distinction between descriptive enemy-identification and coercive moral recategorization.

**Finding 3: repetition and institutions stabilize the system.** ADL-001-E/F connect repeated designation and coercive institutions (`mi-ne-su`) to growth in `wi-ra` and `wi-ra-ki`. This gives the first explicit corpus test of how designation becomes durable rather than episodic.

**Finding 4: ordinary persons enter through structural pressure, not necessarily prior fanaticism.** ADL-001-G bridges Browning's `su-li` / `go-su` analysis to designation systems: ordinary persons can bind themselves to coercive power when structural force and legitimized enemy-designation converge.

**Finding 5: `wife'atofeka` can stabilize legitimized enemyhood.** ADL-001-H links enemy-designation to the self-legitimating closure established in WFE-001/002. This gives a formal answer to the contradiction-survival question: some labels persist not because they are never challenged, but because the challenge-space is already bounded.

## ADL-003 — Purification, Sacrificial Discharge, and Scapegoat Logic (S897–S904)

**Batch:** ADL-003  
**Source:** Third-pass structural battery for adversarial designation systems. Extends ADL-001/002 into communal fault-assignment, purification language, sacrificial discharge, worthy-victim reframing, and the repetition logic of scapegoating.  
**Entries:** S897–S904 · **New vocabulary:** none (all compositional)

**Key tests:**
- `de-su` source-assignment with `go-zo-li` / `go-o-li` — fault relocated to the target rather than the collective
- `ka-no-de-su  lo-ne-su` — purification of the social network as the political effect claimed by designation
- `ka-de` + `no ne de-su` — sacrifice framed as clearing communal fault
- `vo-de-zo-li` reused in sacrificial register — the harmed target reconstructed as a worthy victim

- structural correction: the fault lies in the network, not in the singled-out person

**Central claim:** Once legitimacy-conferring enemy-designation relocates communal fault onto a target, coercion can be narrated as purification and sacrifice as repair. ## KAF-001 — Kafka, The Trial: Arrest Before Explanation (S913–S915)

**Batch:** KAF-001  
**Source:** Franz Kafka, *The Trial* (opening chapter), focusing on accusation, detention, and opaque procedure. Tests whether Tonesu can separate accusation, prohibition, and adjudication instead of collapsing them into a single atmospheric event.  
**Entries:** S913–S915 · **New vocabulary:** none (all compositional)

**Key tests:**
- `si [la-X ne de-su]` — accusation rendered as a signaled fault-claim rather than a proved fault
- `ka-ko` in legal-custodial register — detention as deliberate containment
- `wi-fe-ka [la-X ka-ki-de]` — arrest rendered as prohibited departure, not physical incapacity
- `no si [la-X ne de-su] / ka-to-fe lo-X` — withheld explanation paired with already-operative judgment

**Central claim:** The opening of *The Trial* is structurally about sequence, not evidence. In Kafka's scene, accusation, prohibition, and adjudication arrive before intelligible fault is made available to the target. Tonesu can make that sequence explicit by separating fault-signal, containment, departure-prohibition, and judgment into distinct operators.

---

### S913 — KAF-001-A

**Source:** "Someone accuses Josef K.; the apparatus detains him."

**Tonesu:** `la-ze  si  [la-na Yosef  ne  de-su] ;  la-o-ka-su  ka-ko  lo-na Yosef`

**Written:** `laze si [lana Yosef ne desu] ; laokasu kako lona Yosef`

**Gloss:** [a-particular-other] signals [Yosef IS fault] ; [state-apparatus] deliberately-contains [Yosef]

**Natural reading:** Someone signals that Yosef is at fault; the state apparatus detains Yosef.

**Notes:** The batch starts with accusation as **signal**, not proof: `si [la-na Yosef ne de-su]` says the apparatus of accusation is already active without yet granting public intelligibility or adjudicated certainty. `de-su` is used predicatively here in the fault/wrong register. `ka-ko` reuses the established control/containment compound in a custodial register: detention is rendered as deliberate containment rather than a separate lexical primitive. The reduced proper-name form follows the KNM-001 precedent `la-na Derek`: the role-marker attaches to `na`, while the protected identifier remains a separate token. The source's longer clause about having done nothing wrong is left implicit so the structural pressure stays on the accusation-to-detention sequence.

---

### S914 — KAF-001-B

**Source:** "You may not leave; you are under arrest."

**Tonesu:** `wi-fe-ka  [la-na Yosef  ka-ki-de] / la-o-ka-su  ka-ko  lo-na Yosef`

**Written:** `wifeka [lana Yosef kakide] / laokasu kako lona Yosef`

**Gloss:** [rule-forbidden] [Yosef deliberately-departs] / [state-apparatus] deliberately-contains [Yosef]

**Natural reading:** Yosef may not leave / the state apparatus is containing him.

**Notes:** This is the key Kafka sentence in Tonesu form. `wi-fe-ka [la-X ka-ki-de]` cleanly separates **normative prohibition** from **physical inability**, following PERM-001. `ka-ki-de` is the deliberate-departure form already established in permission/capability work. The second clause then states the custodial fact: containment is the mechanism by which the prohibition is made real. That yields a more precise analysis than a single English verb like "arrest," which bundles rule, force, and status together.

---

### S915 — KAF-001-C

**Source:** "We are not here to tell you the fault; the proceedings are already under way."

**Tonesu:** `la-o-ka-su  no  si  [la-na Yosef  ne  de-su] / la-o-ka-su  ka-to-fe  lo-na Yosef`

**Written:** `laokasu no si [lana Yosef ne desu] / laokasu katofe lona Yosef`

**Gloss:** [state-apparatus] NOT signals [Yosef IS fault] / [state-apparatus] deliberately-judges [Yosef]

**Natural reading:** The state apparatus does not disclose that Yosef is at fault / the state apparatus is already judging Yosef.

**Notes:** This renders the most important Kafka asymmetry: explanation is withheld while adjudication is already operative. `no si [la-na Yosef ne de-su]` is not a denial that an accusation exists; it is a refusal to disclose or restate it. `ka-to-fe` then names the underway process as deliberate adjudication, not neutral inquiry. The pair shows why Kafka's opening feels structurally oppressive: the person is already inside the machinery of judgment before the content of the supposed fault becomes available.

**Entries:** S913–S915 · **New vocabulary:** none

## NAM-001 — Reduced Bare-Name Orthography (S916–S921)

**Batch:** NAM-001  
**Source:** naming-orthography stress test prompted by KAF-001 and KNM-001 precedent. Tests whether reduced bare-name NPs should keep the identifier as a separate token after `na` for human readability.  
**Entries:** S916–S921 · **New vocabulary:** none

**Key tests:**
- `la-na Name` in agent position
- `lo-na Name` in patient position
- `ne-na Name` in relational/possessive position
- reduced bare-name form inside an embedded clause
- `he na Name` and bare `na Name!` contrast

**Working claim:** The reduced bare-name form is easiest to parse when the role-marker scopes over `na`, while the protected identifier remains a separate token. That yields `la-na Derek`, `lo-na Derek`, `ne-na Derek`, `he na Derek`, rather than fused identifier notation like `la-na-Derek`.

---

### S916 — NAM-001-A

**Source:** "Derek built the machine."

**Tonesu:** `la-na Derek  ka-be  lo-mu`

**Written:** `lana Derek kabe lomu`

**Gloss:** agent-name Derek deliberate-build patient-machine

**Natural reading:** Derek built the machine.

**Notes:** First attestation of the reduced bare-name form in agent position with the identifier kept as a separate token. This is the KNM-001 pragmatic reduction made explicit as a live corpus sentence rather than a note: `la-` scopes over `na`, but `Derek` remains visually intact.

---

### S917 — NAM-001-B

**Source:** "I saw Derek."

**Tonesu:** `la-mi  se  lo-na Derek`

**Written:** `lami se lona Derek`

**Gloss:** agent-I perceive patient-name Derek

**Natural reading:** I saw Derek.

**Notes:** Patient position behaves the same way as agent position: `lo-na Derek` is readable as patient-scoped name phrase, and the identifier remains separate after `na`. This matters because the patient form is where fused written outputs like `lonaDerek` feel most visually misleading.

---

### S918 — NAM-001-C

**Source:** "Mira saw Derek's machine."

**Tonesu:** `la-na Mira  se  lo-mu  ne-na Derek`

**Written:** `lana Mira se lomu nena Derek`

**Gloss:** agent-name Mira perceive patient-machine relation-name Derek

**Natural reading:** Mira saw Derek's machine.

**Notes:** This extends the same orthography to relational/possessive `ne-na Derek`. The earlier Exodus material used the same structural role in fused notation (`ne-na-Yitro`); NAM-001 retests it in the spaced reduced form and confirms that the identifier remains easier to track as a separate token.

---

### S919 — NAM-001-D

**Source:** "I know that Derek built the machine."

**Tonesu:** `la-mi  to  [la-na Derek  ka-be  lo-mu]`

**Written:** `lami to [lana Derek kabe lomu]`

**Gloss:** agent-I know [agent-name Derek deliberate-build patient-machine]

**Natural reading:** I know that Derek built the machine.

**Notes:** Embedded-clause position does not create any new pressure. The reduced form remains clear under clause embedding: the boundary problem is visual, not grammatical, and the spaced identifier solves that visual pressure without requiring a new bracket type or role-marker redesign.

---

### S920 — NAM-001-E

**Source:** "Derek, speak."

**Tonesu:** `he  na Derek,  ka-si`

**Written:** `he na Derek, kasi`

**Gloss:** vocative name Derek deliberate-speak

**Natural reading:** Derek, speak.

**Notes:** Vocative position strongly favors the spaced identifier visually. `he na Derek` is immediately legible as direct address in a way that `he na-Derek` or `henaDerek` is not. This sentence confirms that the reader-facing argument for the spaced form is strongest in the speech-act frame, not only in ordinary predicate clauses.

---

### S921 — NAM-001-F

**Source:** "Derek!"

**Tonesu:** `na Derek!`

**Written:** `na Derek!`

**Gloss:** name Derek

**Natural reading:** Derek!

**Notes:** Bare exclamatory NP keeps the same convention: `na` introduces the identifier, but does not fuse with it. Together S920 and S921 reconfirm the CVP-001 distinction in the reader-friendly orthography: `he na Derek` is a vocative speech act, while `na Derek!` is the name uttered with force.
