## Why this packet exists

The newsroom and secondary-report trail for airline employee vaccine policies was weaker than the project standard required. This SEC filing is an official, durable, public source that cleanly preserves the minimum rule structure needed to file a United employer-side specimen: an employee vaccine requirement, a U.S.-based scope limit, and an exemption lane. It does not preserve the full consequence ladder, so this packet also records the source-thinness that shaped COV-ORG-003.

## Operative lines preserved

> "During the third quarter of 2021, the Company implemented a COVID-19 vaccine requirement for U.S.-based employees, subject to certain exemptions."

> "For example, the Company is involved in litigation relating to its vaccination requirements for employees."

## Batch use

These preserved lines support the three filed claims in COV-ORG-003:

- vaccination is stated as an employer work condition
- the rule is explicitly limited to U.S.-based employees
- exemptions are real but left unspecified in the filing language

The litigation sentence is preserved as corroborating context that the company treated employee vaccination requirements as a real legal and operational policy surface, not merely an aspirational statement.
