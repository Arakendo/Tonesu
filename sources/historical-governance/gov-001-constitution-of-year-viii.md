## Status

This packet settles the authority side of the Napoleonic administrative-state specimen around the Constitution of Year VIII. ## Why this packet exists

`GOV-001` needs an authority-defining text that states who governs, who appoints, who drafts administrative rules, who executes them, and how local administration is subordinated upward. The Constitution of Year VIII does that before the law of `28 pluviôse an VIII` adds the prefectural territorial implementation detail.

It is therefore the cleanest starting point for the formal state-bureaucracy control specimen: the executive chain is already explicit before the prefect law supplies the lower ladder.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. The First Consul holds the main nomination and revocation power over the governing chain

Article 41:

- `Le Premier consul promulgue les lois ; il nomme et révoque à volonté les membres du Conseil d'État, les ministres, les ambassadeurs et autres agents extérieurs en chef, les officiers de l'armée de terre et de mer, les membres des administrations locales et les commissaires du gouvernement près les tribunaux.`

Structural value:

- makes the executive center explicit
- ties local administration and governing agents directly to appointment from above

### 2. The government both proposes laws and makes the regulations necessary for their execution

Article 44:

- `Le gouvernement propose les lois, et fait les règlements nécessaires pour assurer leur exécution.`

Structural value:

- distinguishes law proposal from later execution while still chaining them through one executive surface
- gives `GOV-001` a clean rule-to-execution clause before the prefectural law is added

### 3. The Council of State drafts projects and resolves administrative difficulties under the consuls

Article 52:

- `Sous la direction des consuls, un Conseil d'État est chargé de rédiger les projets de lois et les règlements d'administration publique, et de résoudre les difficultés qui s'élèvent en matière administrative.`

Structural value:

- shows the administrative state as rule-drafting and difficulty-resolving, not only commanding
- gives the specimen a distinct administrative-intelligence layer above territorial execution

### 4. Ministers procure execution of laws and administrative regulations

Article 54:

- `Les ministres procurent l'exécution des lois et des règlements d'administration publique.`

Structural value:

- provides the central execution link between the executive center and local administration
- makes ministerial office operative rather than ceremonial

### 5. Local administrations are subordinated to ministers

Article 59:

- `Les administrations locales établies soit pour chaque arrondissement communal, soit pour des portions plus étendues du territoire, sont subordonnées aux ministres.`

Structural value:

- gives `GOV-001` its cleanest constitutional subordination clause
- sets up the later prefectural law as a specification of an already constitutional reporting and execution chain

### 6. Government acts depend on ministerial signature, and ministers are responsible for non-execution

Articles 55 and 72:

- `Aucun acte du gouvernement ne peut avoir d'effet s'il n'est signé par un ministre.`
- `Les ministres sont responsables ... 2° De l'inexécution des lois et des règlements d'administration publique ...`

Structural value:

- ties validity and responsibility to office-bearing administrative mediation
- keeps `GOV-001` from collapsing into a pure one-man-command reading
