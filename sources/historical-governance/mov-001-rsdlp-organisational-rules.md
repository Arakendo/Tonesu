# MOV-001 — RSDLP Organisational Rules Packet

## Status

Selected packet. This is the current best authority-defining text for `MOV-001`.

This packet settles the authority side of the early Bolshevik apparatus specimen around the `Organisational Rules of the Russian Social-Democratic Labour Party` adopted at the Party's Second Congress in 1903.

- Historical setting: adopted at the Party's Second Congress
- Date: 1903

## Why this packet exists

`MOV-001` needs a party-statute surface that shows how authority is arranged inside the apparatus before the later party-state fusion is taken for granted.

The 1903 rules do that directly enough for this track: they define congress supremacy, identify the party centres, assign practical direction to the central committee, and make central decisions binding on party organisations.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. The congress is the supreme organ of the party

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `The supreme organ of the Party is the Party Congress.`

Structural value:

- gives `MOV-001` a direct authority-defining peak institution
- keeps the party apparatus from collapsing into an unstructured revolutionary mass

### 2. The Party Council is the highest party institution between centres and coordinates them

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `The Party Council is the highest Party institution.`
- `Its task is to reconcile and co-ordinate the activity of the Central Committee and the editorial board of the Central Organ ...`

Structural value:

- shows that the apparatus has an explicit coordinating centre, not only a congress and local committees
- preserves a visible relation between ideological and practical centres

### 3. The Central Committee directs practical activity across the party

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `The Central Committee organises committees, associations of committees and all other Party institutions, and directs their activities ...`
- `... and, in general, co-ordinates and directs all the Party's practical activity.`

Structural value:

- provides the core committee-direction clause for the apparatus side of the case
- makes the central committee more than a symbolic leadership surface

### 4. Central decisions bind all party organisations

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `All decisions by the Central Committee are binding on all Party organisations ...`
- `Apart from the organisations established by the Party Congress, all other Party organisations are subject to approval by the Central Committee.`

Structural value:

- shows how the apparatus claims authority over subordinate bodies
- gives `MOV-001` a clean routed-obedience clause without needing later consolidated-state texts

### 5. Party membership is defined by program acceptance, financial support, and regular assistance under direction

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `A member of the Russian Social-Democratic Labour Party is one who accepts the Party's programme, supports the Party financially, and renders it regular personal assistance under the direction of one of its organisations.`

Structural value:

- gives `MOV-001` a membership-discipline clause rather than only high-level apparatus structure
- shows that party belonging is defined through directed participation, not only assent

### 6. Party organisations must supply information upward

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `Every Party organisation is obliged to supply both to the Central Committee and to the editorial board of the Central Organ all information regarding every aspect of its activity and all its members.`

Structural value:

- adds an explicit upward-reporting requirement inside the apparatus
- shows that central direction is backed by information flow, not only formal supremacy

### 7. Co-option and expulsion require a supermajority

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `For co-opting members and expelling them, a two-thirds majority is needed ...`

Structural value:

- gives the case an internal gatekeeping rule for membership changes
- keeps the apparatus from reading as pure arbitrary command

### 8. Appeals on co-option or expulsion may go to the Party Council

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `Appeal may be made to the Party Council regarding any organisation's decision on co-option or expulsion of members.`

Structural value:

- adds a review path inside the party structure
- strengthens `MOV-001` as an organised apparatus with internal adjudicative routing
