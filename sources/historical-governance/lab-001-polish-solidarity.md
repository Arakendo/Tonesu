## Target structure

- batch code: `LAB-001`
- candidate specimen: Polish Solidarity / NSZZ `Solidarność`
- intended role in track: labor / union governance specimen showing how strike participation becomes representative organization, negotiated legitimacy, and internal governance

## Why this case is next

`LAB-001` gives the track a participatory governance form that is neither straightforward state administration nor corporate charter rule nor purely moral obedience.

It is useful because it can pressure:

- negotiated recognition rather than purely unilateral authority
- worker participation becoming governance rather than only protest
- delegate and commission structures inside a mass labor body
- the distinction between founding demands, legal recognition, and internal rule

This makes it the cleanest phase-2 follow-on after the phase-1 controls.

## Preferred authority-defining source family

Start with the August 1980 founding-agreement family.

Preferred first anchor families:

- Gdańsk / August Agreement protocol of 31 August 1980
- Twenty-One Demands of the Inter-Factory Strike Committee, if the agreement text itself is too compressed to carry the authority claim cleanly

Structural questions for the anchor text:

- whether independent union status is conceded, recognized, or merely negotiated in principle
- how the workers' committee appears as a governing counterparty rather than a crowd
- whether strike rights and representation rights are coupled or separated
- where the labor body stops being a protest organ and starts being an institution

## Preferred action-routing source family

The second text should show how Solidarity governs itself internally once the founding breakthrough occurs.

Best candidate families:

- Solidarity statute / charter text
- national coordinating or commission rules
- inter-factory or regional delegate resolutions that routinize representation, decision, or execution

Structural questions for the second text:

- who speaks for the union and how that authority is delegated
- how regional bodies relate to national bodies
- where delegate, commission, and executive functions are distinguished
- how labor participation becomes organized governance rather than episodic mobilization

### Current best founding-side source family

The first pass now yields a concrete August 1980 source family:

- Polish Wikisource transcription of `21 postulatów Międzyzakładowego Komitetu Strajkowego z 17 sierpnia 1980`: `https://pl.wikisource.org/wiki/21_postulat%C3%B3w_Mi%C4%99dzyzak%C5%82adowego_Komitetu_Strajkowego_z_17_sierpnia_1980`
- Polish Wikisource transcription of `Protokół ustaleń MKS z komisją rządową w Gdańsku (1980)`: `https://pl.wikisource.org/wiki/Protok%C3%B3%C5%82_ustale%C5%84_MKS_z_komisj%C4%85_rz%C4%85dow%C4%85_w_Gda%C5%84sku_(1980)`
- archived `Memory of Poland` / UNESCO listing preserving `The Twenty-One Demands, Gdańsk 1980` as a named documentary item
- IPN Kraków commemorative PDF page: `https://krakow.ipn.gov.pl/pl4/nauka-i-edukacja/edukacja-1/przystanek-historia/107450,Porozumienia-Sierpniowe-40-rocznica-1980-2020-PDF.html`
- paywalled but title-confirming English translation witness: JSTOR / World Affairs, `The Gdansk Agreement: Protocol of Agreement between the Government Commission and the Interfactory Strike Committee Concluded on August 31, 1980 at Gdansk Shipyards`

Why this matters:

- the founding event is easy to identify and date
- the demand-side documentary family is clearly real, not inferential
- the protocol text itself is now publicly visible rather than only inferred from later summaries
- the remaining problem is no longer basic founding-text recovery, but deciding whether the demands or the protocol should serve as the authority-defining packet

Current judgment:

- strong enough to open `LAB-001`
- now selected into a dedicated authority packet
- the Twenty-One Demands remain the immediate pressure-side antecedent, not the selected authority packet

### Current best internal-governance source family

The first pass recovered two distinct governance surfaces:

- later consolidated statute PDFs on official or semi-official surfaces
- a period-fit 1981 congress archive on the official Solidarity site

The controlling governance witness is now selected from the second family:

- official archive page: `REGULAMIN WYBORU WŁADZ NSZZ „SOLIDARNOŚĆ” (39/81)`
- URL: `https://www.solidarnosc.org.pl/dok/1981/09/29/regulamin-wyboru-wladz-nszz-solidarnosc-3981/`
- adjacent transition witness: `Uchwała I KZD ws. udzielenia absolutorium (37/81)`
- corroborative but not primary later texts:
	- `https://www.solidarnosc.org.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/STATUT_NSZZ_Solidarnosc_po_zmianach_na_XXX_KZD_2022_tekst_jednolity-1.pdf`
	- `http://www.solidarnosc.uw.edu.pl/images/statut.pdf`

Why this matters:

- it gives `LAB-001` a governance packet from the actual 1980-1981 formation window
- it shows delegated election of national executive and review bodies directly, without importing later institutional layers
- it resolves the main period-fit caution that attached to relying on the 2006 or 2022 consolidated statutes

Current judgment:

- good enough to treat the 29 September 1981 election regulation as the selected governance-side packet
- the later consolidated statutes remain useful corroborative witnesses for continuity and terminology, but they are no longer the controlling gap
- the official `1 KZD` archive turned out to be the decisive recovery surface
