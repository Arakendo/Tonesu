## Why this case belongs in phase 3

`PAR-001` gives the track a case where force is neither purely private nor simply ordinary state office.

It is useful because it can pressure:

- the shift from tolerated or encouraged party violence to recognized militia status
- force that is adjacent to state authority without collapsing cleanly into it
- loyalty, oath, and ideological affiliation as a basis for organized coercive action
- territorial chains of command below the national leadership level

This makes it the right next complement to `REV-001`, `GOV-001`, `CGV-001`, `REL-001`, and `LAB-001`: it tests how Tonesu handles force adjuncts that are institutionally real before they are fully legible as ordinary administration.
