# MOV-001 — April Theses Packet

## Status

Supporting packet. This is the current best programmatic party-line text for the third `MOV-001` pass.

This packet adds Lenin's `The Tasks of the Proletariat in the Present Revolution` (`April Theses`) as a bridge between the 1903 organisational rules and the later October operational directive.

## Source

- Title: `The Tasks of the Proletariat in the Present Revolution`
- Common name: `April Theses`
- Author: Vladimir Ilyich Lenin
- Date: April 4, 1917
- Public surface: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1917/apr/04.htm`
- Host: Marxists Internet Archive

## Used by

- third-pass programmatic text for `MOV-001`
- referenced from `sources/historical-governance/mov-001-bolshevik-apparatus.md`

## Why this packet exists

`MOV-001` needed one additional surface that states the party line in a pre-insurrection but already anti-parliamentary and anti-Provisional-Government form.

The `April Theses` are the cleanest current packet for that role: they reject support for the Provisional Government, identify the Soviets as the only possible form of revolutionary government, and demand transfer of power away from parliamentary form and toward a republic of Soviets.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. No support for the Provisional Government

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `No support for the Provisional Government ...`

Structural value:

- gives `MOV-001` a sharp anti-government party-line clause before full seizure of power
- keeps the specimen from collapsing the party into the existing state surface

### 2. The Soviets are the only possible form of revolutionary government

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `The masses must be made to see that the Soviets of Workers' Deputies are the only possible form of revolutionary government ...`

Structural value:

- adds the programmatic constitutional claim that links the party line to soviet legitimacy

### 3. Entire state power must pass to the Soviets

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `... we preach the necessity of transferring the entire state power to the Soviets of Workers' Deputies ...`

Structural value:

- bridges party explanation to demanded institutional transfer

### 4. Not a parliamentary republic, but a republic of Soviets throughout the country

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `Not a parliamentary republic ... but a republic of Soviets of Workers', Agricultural Labourers' and Peasants' Deputies throughout the country, from top to bottom.`

Structural value:

- adds the anti-parliamentary regime-form contrast directly
- gives `MOV-001` a state-form clause distinct from the later uprising directive
