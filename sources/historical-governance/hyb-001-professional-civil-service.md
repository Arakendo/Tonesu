# HYB-001 — Law for the Restoration of the Professional Civil Service Packet

## Status

This packet settles the exclusionary-administration side of the specimen around the `Law for the Restoration of the Professional Civil Service` of 7 April 1933.

## Why this packet exists

`HYB-001` needs more than a fusion statute. It also needs an administrative text showing how the fused order sorts personnel, excludes targeted groups, and purges politically unreliable officials from public service.

This law does that directly enough for the track, and the Yad Vashem page preserves a usable English translation of the operative clauses.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. Civil servants may be dismissed even where prior law would not otherwise allow it

Yad Vashem text:

- `To restore a national professional civil service and to simplify administration, civil servants may be dismissed from office in accordance with the following regulations, even where there would be no grounds for such action under the prevailing Law.`

Structural value:

- shows administrative purge authority being detached from ordinary prior-law grounds

### 2. Civil servants not of Aryan descent are to be retired

Yad Vashem text:

- `Civil servants who are not of Aryan descent are to be retired ...`

Structural value:

- gives `HYB-001` a direct classification-and-exclusion clause

### 3. Civil servants lacking assurance of support for the national state can be dismissed

Yad Vashem text:

- `Civil servants whose previous political activities afford no assurance that they will, at all times, give their fullest support to the national State, can be dismissed from the service....`

Structural value:

- adds political reliability as an explicit criterion for removal from office
