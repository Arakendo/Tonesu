## Why this packet exists

`PAR-001` needs a first text that does more than narrate Fascist squad violence after the fact. It needs the legal-incorporation surface that turns the earlier squad force into a recognized militia and states where command, recruitment, and mission are located.

This decree is the cleanest currently recovered authority-defining packet because it does exactly that:

- institutes the militia as a named body
- places it under the Head of Government
- defines its basic security and defense role
- makes recruitment and further organization depend on executive judgment and later regulation
- forbids other party formations with military character once this decree takes effect

That makes it the right first packet for the PAR specimen even though later texts, especially the 1924 decree, regularize the structure more fully.

## Operative clauses located so far

The clauses below are now checked against the Archive page images for issue `19230120_016`, Gazette pages `383-384`.

### 1. The decree institutes a voluntary militia for national security

Article 1:

- `E' istituita una milizia volontaria per la sicurezza nazionale.`

Structural value:

- gives `PAR-001` its cleanest incorporation clause
- turns the force from a party or movement formation into a named state-recognized body

### 2. The militia is under the Head of Government and tasked with internal order in concert with regular forces

Article 2:

- `La milizia per la sicurezza nazionale è al servizio di Dio, della Patria italiana, ed è agli ordini del Capo del Governo.`
- `Provvede, in concorso coi corpi armati per la pubblica sicurezza e con il R. esercito, a mantenere all'interno l'ordine pubblico; prepara e conserva inquadrati i cittadini per la difesa degli interessi dell'Italia nel mondo.`
- it also prepares and keeps citizens organized for defense of Italian interests.

Structural value:

- fixes the primary command relation at the top of the regime
- shows that the militia is neither wholly private nor identical with the ordinary army
- directly supports the hybrid force-adjunct structure central to `PAR-001`

### 3. Recruitment is voluntary from the Fascist militia and depends on executive judgment of capacity and morality

Article 3:

- `Il reclutamento è volontario ... fra gli appartenenti alla milizia fascista fra i 17 e i 50 anni ...`
- admission depends on the judgment of `del Presidente del Consiglio dei ministri o delle autorità gerarchiche da lui delegate` concerning those who `ne posseggano i requisiti di capacità e moralità`.

Structural value:

- shows direct continuity between the Fascist militia and the newly instituted body
- makes political-moral screening explicit at the authority-formation stage
- ties recruitment to executive discretion, not neutral general enlistment

### 4. Organic and disciplinary norms are to be set by regulation under the President of the Council or his delegates

Article 4:

- `Le norme organiche e disciplinari per la costituzione e il funzionamento della milizia saranno stabilite da appositi regolamenti ... dal Presidente del Consiglio o dalle autorità da lui delegate.`

Structural value:

- shows that the first decree is intentionally constituting and framework-setting
- routes detailed organization through later executive regulation rather than statute alone

### 5. Officer appointments and promotions run through royal decree on ministerial proposal

Article 5:

- `Le nomine degli ufficiali e le loro promozioni vengono compiute con Nostro decreto, su proposta dei Ministri per l'interno e per la guerra.`

Structural value:

- inserts the militia into the formal appointment chain of the state
- links the new force to both interior and war ministries rather than only to party command

### 6. In general mobilization, the militia is absorbed into the army and navy in arms

Article 7:

- `In caso di mobilitazione generale o di richiamo parziale dell'esercito e della marina, la milizia fascista viene assorbita dall'esercito e dalla marina in armi ...`

Structural value:

- makes the militia's relation to regular war mobilization explicit from the start
- helps explain why the later 1924 decree could deepen rather than invent the armed-state link

### 7. The militia is funded through the Interior Ministry budget

Article 8:

- `Le spese per l'istituzione e il funzionamento della milizia per la sicurezza nazionale sono a carico del bilancio del Ministero dell'interno.`

Structural value:

- gives the new body an immediate state-funding channel
- strengthens the case that this is institutional incorporation, not only permission

### 8. Other party military formations are forbidden once the decree takes effect

Articles 9-10:

- `Dall'entrata in vigore del presente decreto tutte le altre formazioni a carattere o inquadramento militare di qualsiasi partito non sono permesse.`
- the decree is to be presented to Parliament for conversion and enters into force on `1° febbraio 1923`.

Structural value:

- clarifies that the new militia is meant to monopolize permitted party-style military organization
- gives `PAR-001` a sharp closure clause against rival partisan armed formations
- fixes the visible transition point used in later locator sources
