### Authority-side source recovered and selected

Current best public authority-side surface:

- John Shaw, *Charters Relating To The East India Company From 1600 To 1761* (Madras Government Press, 1887)
- Internet Archive item: `https://archive.org/details/charters-relating-to-the-east-india-company-from-1600-to-1761`
- direct PDF: `https://archive.org/download/charters-relating-to-the-east-india-company-from-1600-to-1761/Charters%20relating%20to%20the%20East%20India%20Company%20from%201600%20to%201761.pdf`
- selected packet: `sources/historical-governance/cgv-001-1600-charter.md`

What this surface currently provides:

- direct charter-body wording for the 31 December 1600 charter rather than only an abstract
- incorporation of the company as a body politic and corporate
- a first and present governor with named committees and continuing succession
- internal lawmaking power for company government
- punishment power by imprisonment and fines
- sole trade privilege for the charter term

Current judgment:

- now selected as the current authority-defining packet
- materially stronger than the earlier 1817 abstract surface
- now image-checked against the Archive page images for printed pages 3, 4, 7, 8, 11, and 12

### Governance-side candidate recovered

Current best governance-side surface:

- 53 Geo. III c. 155 in the same source family
- Wikisource page: `https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Collection_of_Charters_and_Statutes_relating_to_the_East_India_Company/53Geo3_c155`
- selected packet: `sources/historical-governance/cgv-001-1813-governance-act.md`

Why it is currently stronger than the charter side:

- it is presented as full statutory text rather than only an abstract
- it directly states that British territories in India remain in the possession and under the government of the company, subject to superintendence, direction, and control
- it routes licensing through the Court of Directors with Board of Commissioners oversight
- it requires rules, regulations, and special circumstances to be recorded in the books of the governing bodies in licensing cases
- it ties company authority to territorial administration rather than to trade privilege alone

Current judgment:

- strong candidate for the action-routing / governance text
- now selected in a dedicated packet for current use
- now image-checked against the Commons-Wikisource page images for pages 138, 139, 140, 141, 142, and 144
- could still be upgraded later if a cleaner or earlier transition text proves structurally better
