## Status

This packet grounds the Jesuit obedience side in the 1838 public-domain volume `Institutum Societatis Jesu : ordinationes praepositorum generalium et instructiones ad provinciales et superiores societatis`. The current working surface is the Internet Archive OCR text and PDF, and the two operative `REL-001` clauses below have now also been checked directly against the Archive page images.

## Why this packet exists

`REL-001` needed a second text that shows how Jesuit legitimacy becomes operative hierarchy: evaluation, obedience, provincial oversight, mission assignment, removal, and upward reporting. This volume is the first recovered public-domain surface in the repo that does that work directly.

Unlike the *Formula of the Institute*, which states the order's authority structure in compact founding form, the `Institutum` instructions show the machinery of internal command and supervision in motion.

## Operative clauses recovered so far

### 1. The relevant section is explicitly addressed to provincials and superiors

Recovered OCR heading:

- `AD PROVINCIALES ... SUPERIORIBUS SOCIETATIS.`

Structural value:

- the packet is not merely devotional or descriptive
- it is overtly addressed to governance officers inside the order

### 2. Obedience, subordination, and judgment are explicit evaluation criteria

Recovered OCR text:

- `... promoveat; de obedientia et subordinatione ad suos Superiores, et de proprio judicio.`
- `Obediens est, et suis Superioribus subordinatus, nec proprii judicii ...`

Structural value:

- obedience is treated as an administratively examinable condition, not only a spiritual ideal
- the contrast with `proprio judicio` makes subordination part of governance screening

### 3. Provincials must report reasons for changing a superior to the General and propose successors through consultative review

Recovered OCR text:

- `Cum de Superioris alicujus mutatione est, primum causam Praeposito Generali exponat Provincialis ...`
- `Deinde re prius cum Consultoribus examinati, subjiciat quis cuique succedere possit ...`

Structural value:

- makes internal personnel change a routed process rather than a local unilateral act
- shows hierarchy plus consultation: provincial, consultors, superior general
- gives `REL-001` a clean internal-review / succession clause

### 4. Provincials must monitor how superiors govern and inform the General if removal is needed

Recovered OCR text:

- `Superiores, Provincialis diligenter cognoscat ...`
- `... si opus fuerit, de eo removendo certiorem faciat Generalem Praepositum.`

Page-image verification:

- Archive page image `n284.jpg`, printed page `284`, `Instructio XI`
- visible wording on the scan: `... si opus fuerit, de tali Superiore amovendo ab officio nos certiores faciat.`

Structural value:

- oversight flows upward through reporting, not merely through local correction
- removal is formalized inside the order's hierarchy
- this is a strong non-state analogue to administrative supervision and review

Hardening note:

- the page image confirms the conservative reading already used in the corpus: if removal from office becomes necessary, the provincial informs the Superior General
- it still does **not** justify a sharper evaluative paraphrase such as "the superior is judged unfit"; the clause is about reporting and removal, not a fuller theory of incompetence

### 5. Mission assignment is time-bounded, purposeful, and operationalized through rural instruction work

Recovered OCR text:

- `Quamvis ad aliquam missionem unius vel alterius mensis destinari poterunt ...`
- `... ut per villas et pagos doctrinam Christianam tradant, et alia hujusmodi ministeria obeant.`
- `... declarent sibi hoc unum esse propositum, ut Doctrinam Christianam tradant, Confessiones audiant, pacificationes procurent, etc.`

Page-image verification:

- Archive page image `n287.jpg`, printed page `287`, `Pro Missionibus`
- visible wording on the scan: `... declarent sibi hoc unum esse propositum, ut Doctrinam Christianam tradant, Confessiones audiant, pacificationes procurent, etc.`

Structural value:

- gives `REL-001` explicit action-routing through assignment to mission work
- shows obedience not only as static subordination but as deployable labor-routing
- secures a narrower corpus-safe paraphrase: routed mission work includes transmitting Christian doctrine, not merely generic travel or presence

Hardening note:

- the page image resolves the earlier OCR page-number confusion; the operative mission line is on printed page `287`, not the corrupted `387` appearance in the OCR excerpt
- the strongest directly verified claim here is not merely that doctrine may be taught on missions, but that the missionaries are to declare this as part of their stated purpose on arrival

### 6. Acceptance is expected with submission of judgment, and reasons may remain undisclosed

Recovered OCR text:

- `... judiciorumque submissione, sine curiosa cujusquam investigatione exceptum iri speramus ...`
- `... saepe eventurum sit, ut causae ... ob multa et gravia impedimenta evulgari a nobis non possint, nec debeant.`

Structural value:

- demonstrates the order's distinction between binding governance decisions and full explanatory transparency
- pressures the track's questions about obedience, review, and what must or need not be publicly disclosed
