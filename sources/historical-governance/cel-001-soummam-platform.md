# CEL-001 — Soummam Platform Packet

## Status

Selected packet. This is the current best CEL-001 structural witness.

This packet uses the `Plate-forme de la Soummam` as reproduced by Kabyle.com, with the official Algerian government page as the public anchor to the Soummam platform page.

## Source

- Title: `Plate-forme de la Soummam`
- Date: 20 August 1956
- Public surface: `https://kabyle.com/document/plate-forme-de-la-soummam`
- Supporting official surface: `https://www.premier-ministre.gov.dz/fr/post/plateforme-de-la-soummam-20-aout-1956`

## Used by

- structural witness for `CEL-001`
- referenced from `sources/historical-governance/cel-001-fln-cells-algeria.md`

## Why this packet exists

`CEL-001` needs a witness that makes FLN authority legible as a clandestine but organised chain rather than as a loose anti-colonial mood.

The Kabyle reproduction does that directly enough for this track: it describes the FLN as clandestine, unified, centrally guided, installed organically throughout the country, and supported by a military organisation that is itself unified and strategic.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. The FLN is clandestine but uniquely national

Kabyle.com text:

- `Le FRONT DE LIBÉRATION NATIONALE, malgré son activité clandestine, est devenu aujourd’hui l’unique organisation véritablement nationale.`

Structural value:

- gives the case a clandestine but nationally authoritative organisation surface

### 2. The FLN's political organisation is effective because it bans personal power and affirms collective direction

Kabyle.com text:

- `Le bannissement du pouvoir personnel et l’instauration du principe de la direction collective ...`

Structural value:

- shows internal command as collective rather than charismatic

### 3. The FLN is a revolutionary organisation, not an anarchic revolt

Kabyle.com text:

- `C’est une révolution organisée et non une révolte anarchique.`

Structural value:

- makes the movement legible as organised authority, not mere disturbance

### 4. The FLN must remain the unique guide of the revolution and realize command unity in an état-major

Kabyle.com text:

- `Le meilleur moyen d’y parvenir, c’est de maintenir le FLN comme guide unique de la Révolution Algérienne ... réaliser l’unité de commandement dans un état-major ...`

Structural value:

- gives the case a direct command-unity clause

### 5. The FLN must be installed organically throughout the country

Kabyle.com text:

- `Installer organiquement le FLN dans tout le pays, dans chaque ville, village, mechta, quartier, entreprise, ferme, université, collège, etc.`

Structural value:

- gives the case a network-routing clause that matches cell-based spread

### 6. The official summary page points back to the Soummam platform as the foundational text

Official page text:

- `Plateforme de la Soummam 20 Août 1956`

Structural value:

- anchors the witness in the Algerian government's own presentation of the platform
