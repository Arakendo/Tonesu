# MOV-001 — Advice of an Onlooker Packet

## Status

Selected packet. This is the current best action-routing text for `MOV-001`.

This packet settles the operational side of the early Bolshevik apparatus specimen around Lenin's `Advice of an Onlooker`, written on October 8 (21), 1917.

## Source

- Title: `Advice of an Onlooker`
- Author: Vladimir Ilyich Lenin
- Date: October 8 (21), 1917
- Public surface: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1917/oct/08.htm`
- Host: Marxists Internet Archive

## Why this packet exists

`MOV-001` needs one directive-side surface showing how Bolshevik political line becomes coordinated operative action before later state consolidation.

`Advice of an Onlooker` is the cleanest current first-pass packet because it explicitly bridges soviet-power claims to armed uprising and then names the forces, points, and operational priorities that must be coordinated.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. Transfer of power to the soviets means armed uprising

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `It is clear that all power must pass to the Soviets.`
- `... in practice the transfer of power to the Soviets now means armed uprising.`

Structural value:

- gives `MOV-001` a direct bridge from constitutional-political claim to operative revolutionary action
- keeps the case in the pre-consolidation window the track wanted

### 2. The three main forces must be combined

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `Our three main forces-the fleet, the workers, and the army units-must be so combined ...`

Structural value:

- shows the apparatus as a coordinating centre rather than mere abstract doctrine
- pressures collective action and command without yet defaulting to a regular state chain

### 3. The combined forces must seize and hold key communications and transport points

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `... to occupy without fail and to hold at any cost: (a) the telephone exchange; (b) the telegraph office; (c) the railway stations; (d) and above all, the bridges.`

Structural value:

- makes operational routing concrete
- shows how revolutionary authority is enacted through communications and movement control

### 4. The best workers are to be formed into small detachments

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `The most determined elements (our "shock forces" and young workers, as well as the best of the sailors) must be formed into small detachments ...`

Structural value:

- preserves a direct small-unit organisation clause
- gives the first `MOV-001` pass a clean action-formation sentence without needing to over-translate the whole uprising document
