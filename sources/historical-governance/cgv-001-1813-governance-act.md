## Why this packet exists

This act is the current best governance-side text for `CGV-001` because it shows the East India Company not merely as a trade corporation but as a territorial governing body operating under a layered structure of company authority, Crown/Parliamentary superintendence, Board oversight, licensing, promulgation, and record-carry.

It is therefore exactly the kind of transition text the comparative legitimacy track needs: the jump from chartered privilege to administrable governance is visible in the operative clauses themselves.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. Territorial government continues in the Company under superintendence, direction, and control

Image-checked reading from pages 138-139:

- `That the Territorial Acquisitions ... shall remain and continue in the Possession and under the Government of the said United Company, subject to such Powers and Authorities for the Superintendance, Direction and Controul over all Acts, Operations, and Concerns which relate to the Civil or Military Government or Revenues of the said Territories ...`

Image check note:

- verified on pages 138-139 / Commons-Wikisource `Page:.../138` and `Page:.../139`
- page 138 carries the act opening and transition into the territorial clause; page 139 confirms the possession / government / superintendence wording used here

Structural value:

- moves the company beyond mere trade privilege into explicit territorial government
- preserves the split between company possession/government and higher superintendence / direction / control
- gives a clean corporate-governance counterpart to the revolutionary routing clauses in `REV-001`

### 2. Certain public trade access remains open only under the act's regulated structure

Image-checked reading from pages 139-140:

- `... from and after the said Tenth Day of April One thousand eight hundred and fourteen, it shall and may be lawful for any of His Majesty's Subjects, in common with the said United Company, to export ... to all Ports and Places within the Limits herein-before referred to; save and except the Dominions of the Emperor of China ... subject nevertheless to the several Restrictions, Conditions, and Limitations, in this Act contained.`

Image check note:

- verified on pages 139-140 / Commons-Wikisource `Page:.../139` and `Page:.../140`
- page 140 confirms the open-trade clause in its operative form, including the China exception and the closing restriction language

Structural value:

- shows that opening trade does not dissolve the governance structure
- helps distinguish partial relaxation of monopoly from loss of governing competence
- useful for Tonesu pressure around permission, privilege, and controlled access

### 3. Orders in Council and licensing decisions are published, approved, and recorded

Image-checked reading from page 141:

- `Copies of all such Orders in Council ... shall have been published Three Times at least in the London or Dublin Gazette ... and Copies of all such Orders shall be laid before Houses of Parliament ...`
- `... according to such Form as shall hereafter be settled by the said Court of Directors, with the Approbation of the Board of Commissioners for the Affairs of India ...`
- `... the special Circumstances inducing them to give such Directions shall be recorded in the Books of the said Board.`

Image check note:

- verified on page 141 / Commons-Wikisource `Page:.../141`
- the scan confirms the publication-in-Gazette language, the Court of Directors plus Board approbation licensing chain, and the requirement to record the special circumstances in the Board's books

Structural value:

- publication is part of the governance machinery, not an afterthought
- licensing is routed through a company-government / Board-oversight chain
- reasons for exceptional intervention are explicitly recorded

### 4. Persons, arms, and manifests are tracked through company-approved forms and transmitted to governing secretaries

Image-checked reading from page 142:

- `... a true and perfect Manifest of the Cargo ... according to such Form, and subject to such Regulations ...`
- `... a true and perfect List in such Form as shall from Time to Time be settled by the said Court of Directors, with the Approbation of the said Board of Commissioners, specifying and setting forth the Names, Capacities, and Descriptions of all Persons ... and all Arms on board ...`
- `... transmit a Copy of such List to the Secretary of the Court of Directors ...` and in India `... to the Chief Secretary of the Government ...`

Image check note:

- verified on page 142 / Commons-Wikisource `Page:.../142`
- the scan confirms both the manifest/list form language and the copy-transmission route to the Company secretary and the Chief Secretary of the Government in India

Structural value:

- makes administrative record-carry central rather than incidental
- shows company and governmental secretariats operating in one routed information structure
- strong evidence for bureaucratic governance rather than only private commercial privilege

### 5. New duties or taxes in India are invalid until sanctioned, approved, publicly dispatched, and promulgated

Image-checked reading from page 144:

- `That no new or additional Imposition of any Duty, or Tax ... made ... by Authority of the Governor General or Governor in Council ... shall be valid or effectual, until the same shall have been sanctioned by the Court of Directors ... with the Approbation of the said Board of Commissioners ...`
- `Which Sanction and Approbation shall be signified ... by some public Dispatch ...`
- `... all such Regulations, when promulgated in the East Indies ... shall contain express Mention that the same are made with the Sanction of the said Court of Directors, and with the Approbation of the said Board of Commissioners ... and such Mention shall be taken as conclusive Evidence ... in all Courts of Justice.`

Image check note:

- verified on page 144 / Commons-Wikisource `Page:.../144`
- the scan confirms the validity condition, the public-dispatch signification language, and the conclusive-evidence clause after promulgation

Structural value:

- gives a direct publication / validity / promulgation clause inside company governance
- clearly separates local governmental act, company sanction, Board approbation, and judicial effect
- especially useful for `to-fe-su`, `to-fe-su-ki`, and `to-fe-li` pressure in the legitimacy track
