## Why this case is next

`REL-001` gives the track a governance form that is neither revolutionary nor corporate nor straightforwardly state-administrative.

It is useful because it can pressure:

- moral and institutional authority without default state force
- obedience as a routed governance relation rather than mere private virtue
- mission assignment, internal discipline, and review structures
- the difference between spiritual legitimacy and administratively organized command

This makes it a strong counterweight to both `REV-001` and the planned corporate/state cases.
