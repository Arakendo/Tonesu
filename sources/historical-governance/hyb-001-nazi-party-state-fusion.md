## Why this case is next

`HYB-001` is the point in the track where party organisation stops being merely adjacent to the state and becomes legally interwoven with it.

It is useful because it can pressure:

- explicit legal fusion between party and state
- elevation of party leadership into government office
- public-law status for the party itself
- administrative classification and exclusion as a governance surface
- removal of civil servants by racial and political criteria
