## Why this pass exists

The comparison track now has enough recovered controls to state the structural question directly: how authority, reporting, publication, and territorial routing differ when the same kind of burden is carried by a state, a revolutionary committee, a militia, or a clandestine movement.

## Comparison axes used so far

- who holds authority
- how orders move downward

- where public publication matters
- how force is held or delegated
- how territorial force is organized
- how visibility changes from office to cell
- how bodies classify members and exclude non-members
- how bodies recruit or position participants
- how upward reports are carried
- how witness or documentary burden is assigned
- what kind of review surface remains available
- how consequence is routinized through office, record, obedience, or assistance

## Current note

This pass is intentionally small. It is a synthesis slice, not a new source hunt.

The first CMP slice established authority, routing, publication, classification, exclusion, and participation across the initial control set.

The second slice now extends that comparison into force relation, enforcement surface, review-path availability, and bureaucratic routinization.

The third slice now extends it further into upward reporting, documentary witness burden, and ordinary participation below overt command.

That matters because some bodies touch force directly, some through auxiliary or territorial structures, some through purge and exclusion, and some through obedience-bound internal rule.

It also matters because review does not survive equally everywhere: some cases preserve recorded or office-borne challenge surfaces, while others narrow or erase them.

It further matters because reporting and witness burden are not one thing. A written report upward, a promulgated documentary record, a review body, and an ordinary participant's burden to nominate, assist, or occupy a territorial slot are structurally different acts.

The participation axis remains distinct from both force and review: office-holding, delegate status, obedience routing, and cell-like installation are not the same mode even when they help the same institution act effectively.
