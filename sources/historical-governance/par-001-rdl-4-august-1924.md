## Why this packet exists

`PAR-001` needs a second text that is not merely about the existence of the Fascist militia, but about how it is reorganized, regularized, and situated inside the state's coercive chain after the initial incorporation move.

This decree is the right action-routing packet because it does four things directly in one legal surface:

- declares the `MVSN` part of the armed forces of the state
- moves oath and discipline onto the same state-military plane as the army
- fixes a territorial and command structure through zones, groups, legions, cohorts, centuries, and maniples
- places the militia under the President of the Council while also routing key military functions through the War Ministry and ordinary armed-force channels

That makes it stronger for the governance side of `PAR-001` than a purely narrative account of squad violence or a later retrospective history.

## Operative clauses located so far

The clauses below are now checked against the Archive page images for issue `19240830_204`, Gazette pages `3015-3017`.

### 1. The militia is explicitly made part of the state's armed forces, with oath to the king and army discipline

Article 1:

- `La M. V. S. N. fa parte delle forze armate dello Stato. I suoi componenti prestano giuramento di fedeltà al Re e sono soggetti alle stesse disposizioni disciplinari e penali di quelli appartenenti al Regio esercito.`

Structural value:

- directly supports the key PAR transition from semi-official force to state-armed incorporation
- ties oath, discipline, and penal order to the monarchy and army rather than only to party loyalty

### 2. The President of the Council reorganizes the militia through a fixed territorial-command structure

Article 2:

- `Il Presidente del Consiglio dei Ministri, d'accordo coi Ministri per la guerra, per la marina, per le colonie, per le finanze e col comandante generale della milizia, provvede a sistemare l'ordinamento della M. V. S. N. in base ai compiti stabiliti dal presente decreto:`
- `La M. V. S. N. avrà il seguente ordinamento: un comando generale; 12 comandi di zona; 3 comandi di gruppo autonomi di legioni; 95 comandi di legione.`
- `Ogni legione sarà costituita da 3 a 5 coorti. Ciascuna coorte si comporrà di 3 centurie, e ciascuna centuria di 3 manipoli.`

Structural value:

- gives `PAR-001` its clearest force-topology clause
- shows that the militia is not only legalized but territorially and hierarchically routed
- preserves the province-scale or zone-scale organization that makes this a governance text rather than only a symbolic act

### 3. The militia has its own hierarchy corresponding to the other armed forces of the state

Article 3:

- `La M. V. S. N. ha una propria gerarchia, corrispondente a quella delle altre forze armate dello Stato.`
- the article then maps `comandante generale`, `console`, `proconsole`, `seniore`, `centurione`, and `capo manipolo` onto army-equivalent ranks.

Structural value:

- regularizes militia office inside a state-military comparison frame
- helps distinguish charismatic or party-command violence from normalized rank structure

### 4. A permanent active cadre is separated from the rest of the militia

Articles 4-5:

- `Il comandante generale e i comandanti di zona, di gruppo autonomo e di legione, e gli ufficiali e militi ad essi addetti, sono permanentemente in servizio.`
- `Tutti gli altri ufficiali e tutti gli altri militi sono normalmente da considerarsi in congedo.`
- `Gli ufficiali ed i militi della M. V. S. N. prestano servizio volontario ...`

Structural value:

- shows a split between always-active command cadres and larger reserve manpower
- makes the force look more like a routinized institution than a permanently mobilized mob

### 5. Officers are drawn from the reserve categories of the regular armed forces

Articles 6 and 8:

- `Gli ufficiali della M. V. S. N. sono tratti dagli ufficiali delle categorie in congedo del Regio esercito, della Regia marina, della Regia aeronautica ...`
- `Gli ufficiali della M. V. S. N. all'atto del loro incorporamento conserveranno il grado e l'anzianità assoluta loro attribuita ... dalle quali provengono.`

Structural value:

- directly supports the claim that army-linked officers are regularized into militia leadership
- shows institutional importation from the ordinary armed forces rather than wholly separate party command

### 6. Admission is politically screened and routed through militia command channels

Articles 7, 9, and 10:

- `I militi sono tratti dai cittadini del Regno ... che rispondano a speciali condizioni di idoneità fisica, morale e politica ...`
- officer admissions are decided after service and civil-life checks by a commission composed of militia and army officers
- citizen applications to become militi are presented to the legion command with jurisdiction over the applicant's place of residence

Structural value:

- shows that the body is selective, territorialized, and politically screened
- makes the recruitment chain legible below the national leadership level

### 7. The militia is under the orders of the President of the Council, but military training is routed through the War Ministry

Article 11:

- `La M. V. S. N. è agli ordini del Presidente del Consiglio e concorrerà a quei servizi che, volta a volta, egli giudicherà del caso nell'interno del Regno e nelle Colonie.`
- `Alla M. V. S. N. è affidata in modo permanente la istruzione premilitare dell'Esercito ...`
- `Per quanto riguarda la istruzione premilitare dell'Esercito, la M. V. S. N. sarà alla diretta dipendenza del Ministero della guerra e delle autorità militari territoriali.`

Structural value:

- preserves the hybrid structure at the center of `PAR-001`: political executive control plus ordinary military routing
- shows that the militia is not simply parallel to the state, but partly embedded within it

### 8. In war or mobilization, militia personnel and units are routed into ordinary armed-force service

Article 12:

- personnel already subject to military-service obligations are, in mobilization or military call-up, incorporated into the units of the regular army, navy, and air force to which they belong
- militia units formed from personnel exempt from those obligations may still be assigned to state defense tasks inside the kingdom, in the colonies, or near the operating army according to tasks assigned by the War Ministry

Structural value:

- shows how the militia links into broader state war-making channels
- strengthens the case that this decree is about operational routing, not only symbolic recognition

### 9. Financing, execution power, and immediate legal effect are centralized upward

Articles 13-16:

- `Tutte le spese per il funzionamento della M. V. S. N. sono a carico del bilancio della Presidenza del Consiglio dei Ministri.`
- `Il Presidente del Consiglio dei Ministri, di concerto coi Ministri interessati, emanerà ogni disposizione necessaria per l'esecuzione del presente decreto.`
- `Il presente decreto avrà attuazione dalla data della sua pubblicazione nella Gazzetta Ufficiale del Regno.`

Structural value:

- gives a clean funding and implementation chain at the top of the regime
- links militia organization to immediate publication-state validity

### 10. Transitional review allows exceptional retention of current rank under top executive review

Articles 17-18:

- the current cadres of the `MVSN` are to be given immediate arrangement in line with the new decree
- for some officers, rank retention may be exceptionally allowed only after personal examination by the President of the Council, assisted by the War Minister and the commander general of the militia

Structural value:

- captures the decree's regularizing and filtering function after the earlier militia phase
- shows that even transitional exceptions are handled through the top executive-military chain
