# MOV-001 — Introduction to the Resolutions Packet

## Status

Supporting packet. This is the current best fourth-pass conference-resolution witness for `MOV-001`.

This packet uses Lenin's `Introduction to the Resolutions of the Seventh (April) All-Russia Conference of the R.S.D.L.P.(B.)`, published on May 3 (16), 1917, as a public summary of the conference line.

## Source

- Title: `Introduction to the Resolutions of the Seventh (April) All-Russia Conference of the R.S.D.L.P.(B.)`
- Author: Vladimir Ilyich Lenin
- Date: May 3 (16), 1917
- Public surface: `https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1917/may/03.htm`
- Host: Marxists Internet Archive

## Used by

- fourth-pass conference-line text for `MOV-001`
- referenced from `sources/historical-governance/mov-001-bolshevik-apparatus.md`

## Why this packet exists

The April Conference index exposes the resolution set clearly, but the individual resolution page URLs are not all cleanly recoverable from the current public surface. This introduction is a usable witness because it explicitly states what the conference resolutions are telling workers to abandon, transfer, and build.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. Trust in and support of the capitalist government must be abandoned

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `... it is absolutely impossible to find a way out of the deadlock unless the policy of trust in and support of the capitalist government is abandoned.`

Structural value:

- sharpens the anti-support line from the third pass into a conference-resolution summary form

### 2. All state power must pass to the Soviets throughout Russia, from the bottom up

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `The situation is one of unparalleled difficulty. There is one way out and only one-the transfer of all state power to the Soviets of Workers', Soldiers', Peasants', and other Deputies throughout Russia, from the bottom up.`

Structural value:

- extends the soviet-transfer claim from workers' deputies alone to the broader deputy structure named in the conference introduction

### 3. Organisation is required at every factory, regiment, company, and residential block

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `Organise at every factory, in every regiment and every company, in every residential block.`

Structural value:

- adds the concrete distributed organisational imperative from the conference line

### 4. Action by millions is impossible without organisation

Marxists Internet Archive text:

- `... unless you realise this clearly, organisation does not mean anything. And without organisation action by the millions is impossible, success is impossible.`

Structural value:

- gives the fourth pass a mass-action requirement clause rather than only institutional naming
