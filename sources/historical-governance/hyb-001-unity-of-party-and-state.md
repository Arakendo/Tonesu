# HYB-001 — Law to Secure the Unity of Party and State Packet

## Status

Selected packet. This is the current best fusion-side authority text for `HYB-001`.

This packet settles the party-state fusion side of the specimen around the `Gesetz zur Sicherung der Einheit von Partei und Staat` of 1 December 1933.

## Why this packet exists

`HYB-001` needs a compact legal text that does more than establish one-party monopoly. It needs a surface that explicitly fuses party and state, upgrades the party into a public-law body, and places party leadership into the government itself.

This law does that directly enough for the track.

## Operative clauses located so far

### 1. The NSDAP is the bearer of the German state concept and inseparably connected with the state

documentArchiv.de text:

- `Nach dem Sieg der nationalsozialistischen Revolution ist die Nationalsozialistische Deutsche Arbeiterpartei die Trägerin des deutschen Staatsgedankens und mit dem Staat unlöslich verbunden.`

Working English sense:

- `After the victory of the National Socialist revolution, the National Socialist German Workers' Party is the bearer of the German state concept and is inseparably connected with the state.`

Structural value:

- gives `HYB-001` a direct party-state fusion clause rather than mere electoral predominance

### 2. The party is a corporation under public law

documentArchiv.de text:

- `Sie ist eine Körperschaft des öffentlichen Rechts. Ihre Satzung bestimmt der Führer.`

Working English sense:

- `It is a corporation under public law. The Führer determines its statutes.`

Structural value:

- makes the party itself a public-law entity rather than only a private political association

### 3. The Deputy Führer and Chief of Staff of the SA become members of the Reich government

documentArchiv.de text:

- `Zur Gewährleistung engster Zusammenarbeit der Dienststellen der Partei und der SA. mit den öffentlichen Behörden werden der Stellvertreter des Führers und der Chef des Stabes der SA. Mitglieder der Reichsregierung.`

Working English sense:

- `To ensure the closest cooperation between the offices of the Party and the SA with the public authorities, the Deputy Führer and the Chief of Staff of the SA become members of the Reich government.`

Structural value:

- gives `HYB-001` a clean legal interlock between party leadership and state office

### 4. Public authorities must provide legal and administrative assistance to party and SA jurisdiction

documentArchiv.de text:

- `Die öffentlichen Behörden haben im Rahmen ihrer Zuständigkeit den mit der Ausübung der Partei- und SA.-Gerichtsbarkeit betrauten Dienststellen der Partei und der SA. Amts- und Rechtshilfe zu leisten.`

Working English sense:

- `Within the scope of their competence, the public authorities are to provide administrative and legal assistance to the offices of the Party and SA entrusted with party and SA jurisdiction.`

Structural value:

- shows state authorities assisting party jurisdiction rather than merely tolerating it
