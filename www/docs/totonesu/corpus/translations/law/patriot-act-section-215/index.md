# USA PATRIOT Act §215 — Translation Analysis (PAT-001 / PAT-002 / PAT-003 / PAT-004)

**Source:** USA PATRIOT Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005, sec. 106, amending FISA §501(b)(2)(A) and §501(d)
**Batches:** PAT-001, PAT-002, PAT-003, and PAT-004
**Sentences:** S1279–S1300
**New entries:** none

---

## Source text

Core operative language from the 2006 reauthorization:

> "a statement of facts showing that there are reasonable grounds to believe that the tangible things sought are relevant to an authorized investigation ..."

These batches are a structural stress test, not a word-for-word translation. The goal is to expose the places where the statute does its real work:

1. it widens the object class from records/documents to `any tangible things`;
2. it licenses collection on a relation-grade standard (`relevant`) that is weaker than the stronger linkage many readers hear in practice; and
3. after the widening is in place, it tries to narrow downstream use through minimization procedures governing retention and dissemination; and
4. it wraps the existence of the order itself in supervised secrecy, with narrow compliance, attorney, and director-permitted leak paths.

## Sentence analyses

### S1279 — PAT-001-A: Authorized inquiry with widened object class

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-to-ki},  no-wi-fe-ka [la-to-fe-li  ka-ko  lo-si-ko-mu / lo-si-su / lo-a-mu]
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lotoki}, nowifeka [latofeli kako losikomu / losisu / loamu]`

**Natural reading:** When an epistemic authority has official authority over an inquiry, it is permitted to collect documents, archives, or any object.

**Notes:** This is not a literal rendering of the whole clause; it isolates the statute's widened reach. `ka-ko` is used as deliberate containment/collection. The crucial move is `lo-a-mu`: once the rule has moved past `si-ko-mu` and `si-su` into the generalized object class, the law is no longer about records alone.

### S1280 — PAT-001-B: The object-class boundary is unresolved

```
la-wi-fe  no  to-su-ki  lo-to-fe  si-ko-mu  ne  a-mu
```

**Written:** `lawife no tosuki lotofe sikomu ne amu`

**Natural reading:** The rule has not comprehended the boundary between document and object.

**Notes:** This is the first failure line. English statute-drafting smooths over the expansion from records to things by folding them into one phrase. Tonesu does not. `si-ko-mu` and `a-mu` are not the same category, and the statute does not supply a principled boundary for the expansion.

### S1281 — PAT-001-C: Relevance as relation to an authorized inquiry

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-to-ki  /  lo-a-mu  ne  to-ne  lo-to-ki},  no-wi-fe-ka [la-to-fe-li  ka-ko  lo-a-mu]
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lotoki / loamu ne tone lotoki}, nowifeka [latofeli kako loamu]`

**Natural reading:** If an authority has official authority over an inquiry and an object stands in conceptual relation to that inquiry, collecting the object is permitted.

**Notes:** `to-ne` is the key compositional choice for "relevant." The statute does not require the object to be the cause of the inquiry, the proof of the claim, or the necessary condition of understanding. It requires a relation the authority is permitted to count as enough.

### S1282 — PAT-001-D: Relevance is not constitutive grounding

```
to-ne  no  helms  go
```

**Written:** `tone no helms go`

**Natural reading:** Relevance is not identical with constitutive or causal grounding.

**Notes:** This is the decisive pressure sentence. In Tonesu, `go` is the strong linkage term: constitutive, grounding, mechanism-asserting. The PATRIOT clause does not demand `go`-grade connection. It demands `to-ne`-grade relation. That is exactly why the phrase can expand operationally while still sounding disciplined.

### S1283 — PAT-001-E: The statute is categorization machinery, not structural truth

```
la-wi-fe  ne  no-su-to  /  ne  wi-fe'ka-to-fe
```

**Written:** `lawife ne nosuto / ne wife'katofe`

**Natural reading:** The rule is not a self-evident structural truth / it is a rule of imposed categorization.

**Notes:** This parallels CDA-001 S643 and ACA-001 S649. Section 215 does not discover a natural class of collectable things or a natural threshold of relevance; it prescribes both for operational use.

### S1284 — PAT-001-F: Structural verdict

```
la-wi-fe  no  to-su-ki  lo-to-fe  to-ne  ne  go  /  la-wi-fe  no  to-su-ki  lo-to-fe  si-ko-mu  ne  a-mu
```

**Written:** `lawife no tosuki lotofe tone ne go / lawife no tosuki lotofe sikomu ne amu`

**Natural reading:** The rule has not comprehended the boundary between relevance and constitutive connection / and it has not comprehended the boundary between document and object.

**Notes:** The statute widens on both axes at once. It lowers the linkage threshold from stronger grounding to mere permitted relation, and it widens the object class from records to any tangible thing. That double widening is the whole controversy.

## PAT-002 — USA PATRIOT Act §215: Presumptive Relevance Ladder (S1285–S1289)

**Batch:** PAT-002  
**Source:** The presumptive-relevance clause inside the same Section 215 amendment: things are presumptively relevant if they pertain to the target, the target's activities, or a person in contact with / known to the target.  
**Entries:** S1285–S1289 · **New vocabulary:** none

---

## Source text focus

This second PAT batch isolates the statute's follow-on move after PAT-001.

The first clause says the government must show reasonable grounds to believe the sought things are relevant to an authorized investigation. The next clause then supplies three category-rings that are treated as presumptively relevant:

1. the target itself,
2. the target's activities, and
3. persons in relation to the target.

The key structural pressure is that the statute no longer asks for a fresh showing of relevance in each case. It supplies a propagation rule.

---

## Vocabulary framework

| Form | Reading | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| `ka-to-ki` | investigate / study deliberately | attested verbal use of W020; used here for inquiry directed at a person |
| `ro-~to-go` | on probable-cause-grade grounds | reuses LGL-001's `~to-go` threshold for justified investigation without full establishment |
| `wi-fe'ka-to-fe` | law as imposed categorization | same legal meta-pattern as PAT-001; now used for presumption rather than bare relevance |
| `si-ko-mu` | record / document | the most legible object class for the presumption ladder |
| `ne` | relation / connection | intentionally broad enough to carry "in contact with" and "known to" without over-specifying the statute's associate relation |
| `[la-ze ka]` | what X does / X's activities | activity-ring rendered compositionally rather than lexicalized as a separate legal noun |

---

## Sentence analyses

### S1285 — PAT-002-A: Target-ring presumption

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-ka-to-ki  lo-ze ; lo-si-ko-mu  ne  lo-ze},  la-wi-fe  wi-fe'ka-to-fe  lo-si-ko-mu  ne  to-ne  lo-to-ki
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lokatoki loze ; losikomu ne loze}, lawife wife'katofe losikomu ne tone lotoki`

**Natural reading:** When an authority officially investigates a person and a record pertains to that person, the rule categorizes the record as relevant to the inquiry.

**Notes:** This is the first ring: target-level pertinence. PAT-001 showed that the statute works at the `to-ne` level. PAT-002 adds that the law is not merely observing relation; it is pre-classifying a whole category as enough.

### S1286 — PAT-002-B: Activity-ring presumption

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-ka-to-ki  lo-ze ; lo-si-ko-mu  ne  [la-ze  ka]},  la-wi-fe  wi-fe'ka-to-fe  lo-si-ko-mu  ne  to-ne  lo-to-ki
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lokatoki loze ; losikomu ne [laze ka]}, lawife wife'katofe losikomu ne tone lotoki`

**Natural reading:** When an authority officially investigates a person and a record pertains to that person's activities, the rule categorizes the record as relevant to the inquiry.

**Notes:** This is already wider than target-identity. The statute shifts from "about the person" to "about what the person does," and still treats the relevance burden as satisfied.

### S1287 — PAT-002-C: Associate-ring presumption

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-ka-to-ki  lo-ze ; la-yu  ne  lo-ze ; lo-si-ko-mu  ne  lo-yu},  la-wi-fe  wi-fe'ka-to-fe  lo-si-ko-mu  ne  to-ne  lo-to-ki
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lokatoki loze ; layu ne loze ; losikomu ne loyu}, lawife wife'katofe losikomu ne tone lotoki`

**Natural reading:** When an authority officially investigates a person, and another person stands in relation to that person, and a record pertains to the second person, the rule categorizes the record as relevant to the inquiry.

**Notes:** The statute's phrase "in contact with, or known to" is left deliberately under-specified. Tonesu preserves that vagueness as bare `ne` rather than laundering it into a richer relation than the source actually gives.

### S1288 — PAT-002-D: Probable-cause targeting propagates through relation

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ka-to-ki  ro-~to-go  lo-ze ; la-yu  ne  lo-ze},  la-wi-fe  wi-fe'ka-to-fe  lo-yu  ne  to-ne  lo-to-ki
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli katoki ro-~togo loze ; layu ne loze}, lawife wife'katofe loyu ne tone lotoki`

**Natural reading:** When an authority investigates a person on probable-cause-grade grounds, and another person stands in relation to that person, the rule categorizes the second person as relevant to the inquiry.

**Notes:** This is the decisive pressure line. The statute begins from `~to`-grade grounds, not full establishment, and then allows the relevance category to travel outward through relation. The widening is therefore not only spatial but epistemic.

### S1289 — PAT-002-E: Structural verdict

```
la-wi-fe  no  to-su-ki  lo-to-fe  {lo-ze ; [la-ze  ka] ; la-yu  ne  lo-ze}  /  ke,  la-wi-fe  ne  wi-fe'ka-to-fe
```

**Written:** `lawife no tosuki lotofe {loze ; [laze ka] ; layu ne loze} / ke, lawife ne wife'katofe`

**Natural reading:** The rule has not comprehended the boundary among target, target-activity, and target-associate / rather, the rule is imposed categorization.

**Notes:** PAT-001 diagnosed the relevance and object-class widenings. PAT-002 shows the operational mechanism that makes the widening durable: once the statute names a target-ring, the burden of independent relevance demonstration is replaced by category membership.

## PAT-003 — USA PATRIOT Act §215: Minimization Procedures and Downstream Use (S1290–S1294)

**Batch:** PAT-003  
**Source:** Section 215's minimization language: the application must enumerate procedures governing retention and dissemination, and later subsections define those procedures as narrowing onward use of information concerning unconsenting persons.  
**Entries:** S1290–S1294 · **New vocabulary:** none

---

## Source text focus

PAT-001 and PAT-002 traced how Section 215 widens intake.

PAT-003 asks the next structural question: once the statute has widened object class and relevance, does it place any real boundary on what happens after collection?

The answer is yes, but only on the back end. The statute does not retract the earlier widening. Instead, it layers a second procedure-language that is supposed to narrow retention and outward disclosure after collection has already been authorized.

That means the law now has a split architecture:

1. broad intake,
2. presumptive relevance propagation, and then
3. downstream minimization.

---

## Vocabulary framework

| Form | Reading | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| `wi-fe-su` | rule-structure / procedure | reused from GDPR-001; ideal for minimization procedures as institutional rule-shape |
| `to-ko` | retained / stored knowledge | W027; used here for retention rather than fresh inquiry |
| `ka-si` | deliberately signal / transmit | reused for dissemination or onward disclosure |
| `ne-vo-wi` | consent | reused from GDPR-001; keeps consent attached to the relevant act |
| `i-zo-li` | specific person | existing person form; sufficient for the protected-person pressure without lexicalizing nationality |
| `de-su` | fault / guilt-status | reused as the cleanest bridge for the crime-evidence exception |

---

## Sentence analyses

### S1290 — PAT-003-A: The application must state minimization procedures

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-ka-to-ki  lo-ze},  la-to-fe-li  ka-si  lo-wi-fe-su  wi  {to-ko  lo-to-si  ne  lo-ze / ka-si  lo-to-si  ne  lo-ze}
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lokatoki loze}, latofeli kasi lowifesu wi {toko lotosi ne loze / kasi lotosi ne loze}`

**Natural reading:** When an authority officially investigates a person, the authority states the procedure-structure for retaining and disseminating that person's information.

**Notes:** This is the application-language turning procedural. The statute no longer speaks only about what may be collected; it also requires the government to describe the downstream handling rule.

### S1291 — PAT-003-B: The order binds the minimization procedures

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  lo-ka-to-ki  lo-ze},  la-wi-fe  ne  wi-fe  lo-wi-fe-su  wi  {to-ko  lo-to-si  ne  lo-ze / ka-si  lo-to-si  ne  lo-ze}
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira lokatoki loze}, lawife ne wife lowifesu wi {toko lotosi ne loze / kasi lotosi ne loze}`

**Natural reading:** When an authority officially investigates a person, the order requires the procedure-structure for retaining and disseminating that person's information.

**Notes:** PAT-003's first narrowing move is not substantive yet but procedural: the order itself is tied to the minimization machinery. Section 215 is trying to make the downstream boundary part of the same legal act that authorizes collection.

### S1292 — PAT-003-C: Without consent, the procedure bounds retention and forbids dissemination

```
go {no  la-i-zo-li  ne  ne-vo-wi  lo-ka-si  lo-to-si  ne  i-zo-li},  la-wi-fe-su  ne  to-fe  lo-to-ko  lo-to-si  ne  i-zo-li  /  ne  wi-fe-ka  [la-to-fe-li  ka-si  lo-to-si  ne  i-zo-li]
```

**Written:** `go {no laizoli ne nevowi lokaci lotosi ne izoli}, lawifesu ne tofe lotoko lotosi ne izoli / ne wifeka [latofeli kasi lotosi ne izoli]`

**Natural reading:** When a person has not consented to the dissemination of that person's information, the procedure is a boundary on retaining that information and a prohibition on disseminating it.

**Notes:** This is the heart of the minimization language. The statute does not undo collection; it imposes a later boundary on how long the information stays stored and whether it can move outward.

### S1293 — PAT-003-D: Fault-signaling information may still be retained and routed onward

```
go {lo-to-si  si  [la-i-zo-li  ne  de-su]},  no-wi-fe-ka  [la-to-fe-li  to-ko  lo-to-si / ka-si  lo-to-fe-li  lo-to-si]
```

**Written:** `go {lotosi si [laizoli ne desu]}, nowifeka [latofeli toko lotosi / kasi lotofeli lotosi]`

**Natural reading:** When information signals that a person stands in fault, the authority may retain the information and signal it onward to the adjudicator.

**Notes:** This compresses the crime-evidence carveout into the legal pattern the corpus already knows best: a signal of fault can override the ordinary no-dissemination direction and move into adjudicative use.

### S1294 — PAT-003-E: Structural verdict

```
la-wi-fe  wi-ra  [la-to-fe-li  ka-ko  lo-a-mu]  /  ke,  la-wi-fe  ne  to-fe  lo-to-ko  lo-to-si  ne  i-zo-li  /  ne  wi-fe-ka  [la-to-fe-li  ka-si  lo-to-si  ne  i-zo-li]
```

**Written:** `lawife wira [latofeli kako loamu] / ke, lawife ne tofe lotoko lotosi ne izoli / ne wifeka [latofeli kasi lotosi ne izoli]`

**Natural reading:** The rule authorizes collecting any object / but then the rule is a boundary on retaining a person's information and a prohibition on disseminating it.

**Notes:** This is the actual shape of the section once PAT-001 through PAT-003 are taken together. The front end widens aggressively; the back end tries to place handling limits after the widened intake is already secured.

1. Section 215's minimization language does not retract the earlier collection widening; it governs what happens after collection has already been authorized.

4. PAT-001 through PAT-003 together reveal a two-stage structure: permissive collection first, controlled afterlife second.

## PAT-004 — USA PATRIOT Act §215: Nondisclosure and Supervised Leakage (S1295–S1300)

**Batch:** PAT-004  
**Source:** Section 215's nondisclosure clause: no person may disclose that the FBI has sought or obtained tangible things under the order, except where disclosure is necessary for compliance, made to an attorney for legal advice or assistance, or otherwise permitted by the Director; downstream recipients inherit the same nondisclosure burden.  
**Entries:** S1295–S1300 · **New vocabulary:** none

---

## Source text focus

PAT-003 asked whether Section 215 narrows downstream handling after collection.

PAT-004 asks the next question: may the recipient even speak about the existence of the order?

The answer is mostly no. The statute converts the fact of compelled production into protected secret, then opens three supervised leak paths:

1. persons necessary to comply with the order,
2. an attorney consulted for legal advice or assistance, and
3. other persons the Director permits.

Those leak paths are not free exits. Secondary recipients inherit the same gag, must be told about it, and non-attorney disclosure channels may themselves be reportable back upward.

That means Section 215 is now doing four different jobs at once: widening collection, presuming relevance, narrowing downstream use, and sealing knowledge that the order exists.

## Sentence analyses

### S1295 — PAT-004-A: The existence of the order becomes protected secret

```
go {la-to-fe-li  ne  wi-ra  [la-ze  ka-ko  lo-a-mu  go-si  lo-wi-fe]},  wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli ne wira [laze kako loamu gosi lowife]}, wifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]`

**Natural reading:** When the authority, by the order, compels a person to produce objects, that person is forbidden to disclose that secret to another person.

**Notes:** The statute's protected content is not the objects themselves but the fact of compelled production. `to-no-si` is the cleanest existing handle for that newly sealed fact.

### S1296 — PAT-004-B: Necessary-compliance and director-permitted disclosure remain allowed

```
go {la-yu  ne  ne-fe  lo-ka-ko  lo-a-mu  go-si  lo-wi-fe  /  la-to-fe-li  ne  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]},  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]
```

**Written:** `go {layu ne nefe lokako loamu gosi lowife / latofeli ne nowifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]}, nowifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]`

**Natural reading:** If another person is necessary for producing the objects under the order, or the authority permits that disclosure, disclosing the secret to that person is permitted.

**Notes:** This compresses two statutory carveouts into one structural permission line: operational necessity and director-approved third-party disclosure.

### S1297 — PAT-004-C: Attorney consultation is a distinct carveout

```
go {la-wi-fe-li  ne  li-pu  go-si  lo-ka-ko  lo-a-mu  go-si  lo-wi-fe},  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-wi-fe-li]
```

**Written:** `go {lawifeli ne lipu gosi lokako loamu gosi lowife}, nowifeka [laze kasi lotonosi lowifeli]`

**Natural reading:** When a law-person's counsel concerns producing the objects under the order, disclosing the secret to that law-person is permitted.

**Notes:** PAT-004 needs the attorney carveout to stay distinct from ordinary compliance assistance. `wi-fe-li` is enough to carry attorney / lawyer as a compositional law-person without introducing new lexicon.

### S1298 — PAT-004-D: Secondary recipients inherit the gag and must be notified

```
go {la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu},  la-yu  ne  wi-fe-ka [la-yu  ka-si  lo-to-no-si]  /  la-ze  ne  wi-fe  lo-ka-si  lo-wi-fe-su  lo-yu
```

**Written:** `go {laze kasi lotonosi loyu}, layu ne wifeka [layu kasi lotonosi] / laze ne wife lokaci lowifesu loyu`

**Natural reading:** If a person discloses the secret to another person, that other person is forbidden to disclose it, and the discloser must state the nondisclosure requirement to that person.

**Notes:** This is where the clause stops being a simple gag and becomes a transmissible secrecy regime. Disclosure is allowed only if the burden is copied forward.

### S1299 — PAT-004-E: Non-attorney disclosure channels may be reportable back upward

```
go {la-to-fe-li  si  [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]},  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-yu  lo-to-fe-li]  /  go {la-ze  wi [ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-wi-fe-li]},  no [la-ze  ne  wi-fe  lo-ka-si  lo-wi-fe-li  lo-to-fe-li]
```

**Written:** `go {latofeli si [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]}, nowifeka [laze kasi loyu lotofeli] / go {laze wi [kasi lotonosi lowifeli]}, no [laze ne wife lokaci lowifeli lotofeli]`

**Natural reading:** If the authority asks to whom the secret was or will be disclosed, naming that person to the authority is permitted; but if the intended disclosure is attorney consultation, the person need not report that consultation to the authority.

**Notes:** This is the oddest structural twist in the subsection: the law supervises leakage channels, but it explicitly leaves attorney consultation outside that reporting duty.

### S1300 — PAT-004-F: Structural verdict

```
go {la-yu  ne  ne-fe  lo-ka-ko  lo-a-mu  go-si  lo-wi-fe  /  la-yu  ne  wi-fe-li  /  la-to-fe-li  ne  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]},  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]  /  go {no [la-yu  ne  ne-fe  lo-ka-ko  lo-a-mu  go-si  lo-wi-fe  /  la-yu  ne  wi-fe-li  /  la-to-fe-li  ne  no-wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]]},  wi-fe-ka [la-ze  ka-si  lo-to-no-si  lo-yu]
```

**Written:** `go {layu ne nefe lokako loamu gosi lowife / layu ne wifeli / latofeli ne nowifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]}, nowifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu] / go {no [layu ne nefe lokako loamu gosi lowife / layu ne wifeli / latofeli ne nowifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]]}, wifeka [laze kasi lotonosi loyu]`

**Natural reading:** If the other person is necessary for compliance, is a law-person, or is otherwise allowed by the authority, disclosure of the secret is permitted; otherwise disclosure of the secret is forbidden.

**Notes:** This is the clean shape of the gag-order branch. The statute does not impose absolute silence. It imposes controlled, supervised leakage under tightly listed channels.

**Key findings:**

1. Section 215 does not merely gather objects and regulate later use; it also seals the fact that the order exists.
2. The secrecy rule is not absolute. It allows operational leakage, attorney consultation, and director-permitted third-party disclosure.

4. PAT-001 through PAT-004 together show the statute's full structure: widened intake, presumed relevance, bounded downstream handling, and controlled secrecy about the order itself.
