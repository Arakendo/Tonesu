**Key structural findings:**

**Finding 1: Kafka's opening pressure is sequential.** The scene is not just "a man is arrested." It is: accusation is signaled, movement is prohibited, and judgment is active before fault is intelligibly disclosed.

**Finding 2: `wi-fe-ka` is the cleanest arrest diagnostic.** S914 shows that the crucial fact is not incapacity but forbidden departure. Arrest is therefore best modeled through the permission/prohibition axis, not through a dedicated arrest lexeme.

**Finding 3: `ka-ko` gives the custodial mechanism.** Detention is deliberate containment by the apparatus. That lets the corpus distinguish the normative state (`wi-fe-ka`) from its institutional enforcement (`ka-ko`).

**Finding 4: Kafka's bureaucracy withholds content while preserving force.** S915 separates non-disclosure (`no si [...]`) from adjudication (`ka-to-fe`). This captures the specifically Kafkaesque structure: procedure is already binding even when intelligibility is not.
