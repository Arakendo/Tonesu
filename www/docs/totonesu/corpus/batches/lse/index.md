## LSE-001 · Lease Entry Notice Clause

<span id="S922"></span>
**S922**
*If the party gives no recorded notice, entry into the room is forbidden.*

<span id="S923"></span>
**S923**
*Once the party gives recorded notice and twenty-four hours pass, entry into the room is permitted.*

<span id="S924"></span>
**S924**
*The contract permits a party to enter the room in order to inspect or repair it.*

**Key structural findings:**

**Finding 1: lease clauses decompose cleanly into deontic state + elapsed-time condition.** English compresses "without 24 hours' notice" into one surface phrase. Tonesu makes the same legal content clearer by splitting no-notice prohibition (S922) from waited-duration permission (S923).

**Finding 2: `si-de` is the right notice form for formal lease register.** The legal notice is not just a spoken signal; it is a signal that has entered the record. `si-de` therefore fits the contract domain better than bare `si`.

**Finding 3: exact legal duration works without special time vocabulary.** `bun mol nu re-ti` gives a literal twenty-four-hour wait in ordinary numeral grammar. No dedicated legal time unit or shorthand is required.

**Finding 4: contract purpose should be explicit.** S924 shows that the legal limit is not merely entry-after-delay, but entry-for-bounded-purpose. The `wi {inspection / repair}` frame captures that directly.
